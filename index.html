Muhammad Arslan Hafeez, He Jp, C L Zhang, Lu Liu

Abstract Annealing is widely applied to reduce porosity and enhance the wear resistance of thermally sprayed metallic coatings; however, its effectiveness is often limited by grain coarsening and brittle phase formation. Here, we present a new approach that directly overcomes these limitations by coupling thermal spraying with the crystallization behavior of a rationally designed amorphous alloy powder (Ni 68.6 Cr 8.7 Nb 3 P 16 B 3.2 Si 0.5 ). Upon annealing at 800°C for 1 h, the metastable Mg 6 Cu 17 Si 7 -type phase present in the as-sprayed coating transforms into a well-refined three-phase microstructure, comprising a ductile Ni-based solid solution and hard Ni 3 P and NbNi 3 intermetallic phases, with 51% reduced intersplat density. This microstructural configuration results in a 21% reduction in COF and an 89% reduction in wear rate. The worn surface of annealed coating demonstrates minor thin grooves, lower fractions of oxides, and no sign of spallation, delamination or cracking, presenting a significant improvement in the wear resistance after annealing. This improvement originates from the three-phase composite microstructure in which the ductile Ni-based solid solution provides plastic deformability and crack resistance, whereas the hard Ni 3 P and NbNi 3 phases provide hardness and load-bearing capacity. Annealed coating exhibits mild abrasive wear mechanism coupled with limited plastic deformation. Exploration of friction stir welding butted thin sheet NiTi with AA6061, AA2024, and AA7075NiTi薄板与AA6061、AA2024及AA7075搅拌摩擦对接焊探索

Brayden Terry, A. Strauss

Friction stir welding was investigated as a method for producing dissimilar butt joints of 1-mm-thick NiTi sheet with three aluminum alloys: AA6061-T6, AA2024-T3, and AA7075-T6. Dissimilar welding of these materials could enable structural integration of shape memory alloys with common aerospace aluminum alloys, but differences in thermal and mechanical behavior make joining difficult. Joining NiTi and AA6061 was successful, while NiTi and AA2024 showed mixed results under tested conditions. NiTi and AA7075 did not join under tested conditions. Difficulty in joining AA2024 and AA7075 came from post-weld cracking attributed to residual stresses and thermal strain due to mismatch in coefficients of thermal expansion combined with lower ductility in the higher-strength aluminum alloys. Tensile testing showed that AA6061–NiTi welds retained moderate strength, with an average ultimate tensile strength of 167.8 MPa and a weld efficiency of 57.9% relative to AA6061-T6, while AA2024–NiTi welds showed lower strength and ductility, with an average tensile strength of 120 MPa and a weld efficiency of 27.6%. Fracture behavior suggests that failure was controlled by the ability of the interface to bear load across the joint. Microscopy revealed a region of dispersed NiTi particles in both AA6061 and AA2024 stir zones. Larger particles showed evidence of aluminum diffusion, while smaller particles were enriched in either Ni or Ti, consistent with formation of Ni-Al and Ti-Al intermetallic compounds. No intermetallic layer was observed on the micron scale at the weld interfaces for either AA6061 or AA2024. A voltage signal analysis method for the welding process using the rice frequency一种利用Rice频率分析焊接过程电压信号的方法

Marek Fidali

This paper investigates the Rice frequency f R , defined as the square root of the ratio of the second to the zeroth spectral moment, as a descriptor of arc voltage signals in gas metal arc (GMA) welding. Short-Time Rice Frequency Analysis (STRFA) was applied to signals recorded in 89 controlled welds, 80 of which were analysed after documented exclusions, covering a reference condition and eleven deliberately introduced process disturbances. The disturbed zones of every weld were annotated from the recorded process channels and from photographs of the specimens. This allowed the disturbed segments to be compared with undisturbed segments of the same weld, which removes the influence of differences between welding sessions. When all windows of whole welds were pooled and compared with the reference welds, the effects of local disturbances were diluted. Within welds, shielding gas interruption decreased the mean f R by 24.5% (5/5 welds), and a 16% increase in welding current increased it by 155% (7/7 welds). The Rice frequency decreased monotonically with root gap (Spearman ρ = −0.90), groove angle (ρ = −0.76) and bevel hole diameter (ρ = −0.59), whereas travel speed and localised oil contamination had no consistent effect. The direction of the response reversed between window widths of about 10 and 50 ms. Long windows reflect the metal transfer frequency, while short windows reflect fast arc voltage fluctuations. The Rice frequency is therefore a computationally inexpensive, two-scale descriptor suitable for online welding monitoring, best used together with other signal features.本文研究了Rice频率f R作为熔化极气体保护电弧（GMA）焊接中电弧电压信号描述量的适用性，该频率定义为二阶谱矩与零阶谱矩之比的平方根。研究对89条在受控条件下焊制的焊缝所记录的信号应用了短时Rice频率分析（STRFA）；按照有记录的排除情况，最终分析了其中80条焊缝，涵盖一种参考工况和十一种人为引入的过程扰动。依据记录的过程通道数据和试样照片，对每条焊缝的扰动区域进行了标注。这使得同一条焊缝内的扰动段与无扰动段能够进行比较，从而消除不同焊接批次之间差异的影响。当汇总完整焊缝的所有时间窗并与参考焊缝进行比较时，局部扰动的影响被稀释。在同一焊缝内进行比较时，保护气体中断使平均f R降低了24.5%（5/5条焊缝），而焊接电流增加16%使其升高了155%（7/7条焊缝）。Rice频率随根部间隙（Spearman ρ = −0.90）、坡口角度（ρ = −0.76）和坡口孔径（ρ = −0.59）的增大而单调降低，而焊接速度和局部油污未表现出一致的影响。在约10 ms与50 ms的窗宽之间，响应方向发生反转。长时间窗反映金属过渡频率，而短时间窗反映快速的电弧电压波动。因此，Rice频率是一种计算成本低、适用于在线焊接监测的双尺度描述量，最好与其他信号特征结合使用。

Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于预测高密度聚乙烯搅拌摩擦焊焊接质量的计算建模与机器学习建模方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation.
