Muhammad Arslan Hafeez, He Jp, C L Zhang, Lu Liu

Abstract Annealing is widely applied to reduce porosity and enhance the wear resistance of thermally sprayed metallic coatings; however, its effectiveness is often limited by grain coarsening and brittle phase formation. Here, we present a new approach that directly overcomes these limitations by coupling thermal spraying with the crystallization behavior of a rationally designed amorphous alloy powder (Ni 68.6 Cr 8.7 Nb 3 P 16 B 3.2 Si 0.5 ). Upon annealing at 800°C for 1 h, the metastable Mg 6 Cu 17 Si 7 -type phase present in the as-sprayed coating transforms into a well-refined three-phase microstructure, comprising a ductile Ni-based solid solution and hard Ni 3 P and NbNi 3 intermetallic phases, with 51% reduced intersplat density. This microstructural configuration results in a 21% reduction in COF and an 89% reduction in wear rate. The worn surface of annealed coating demonstrates minor thin grooves, lower fractions of oxides, and no sign of spallation, delamination or cracking, presenting a significant improvement in the wear resistance after annealing. This improvement originates from the three-phase composite microstructure in which the ductile Ni-based solid solution provides plastic deformability and crack resistance, whereas the hard Ni 3 P and NbNi 3 phases provide hardness and load-bearing capacity. Annealed coating exhibits mild abrasive wear mechanism coupled with limited plastic deformation. Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于预测高密度聚乙烯搅拌摩擦焊焊接质量的计算建模与机器学习建模方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. These findings provide process-driven design guidelines for developing robust HDPE joints and demonstrate how integrating computational modeling with machine learning enables efficient optimization, offering valuable insights into the key characteristics of the FSW process in thermoplastics and supporting its broader adoption in industrial applications.搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）已被公认为一种连接热塑性材料的有效技术。本研究结合计算方法与机器学习方法，对高密度聚乙烯（HDPE）搅拌摩擦焊中的焊接温度、竖直轴向力、材料流动和缺陷形成等特征进行了全面的预测研究，并通过实验结果加以验证。研究建立了三维耦合欧拉–拉格朗日（CEL）数值模型，以模拟不同转速、焊接速度及底部预热条件下的HDPE焊接过程。通过与实验数据对比验证，CEL数值模型在预测孔洞形成、热响应和力学响应方面表现出很高的一致性。结果表明，底面预热能够有效减小温度梯度，并抑制竖直方向的力波动。定量而言，60 °C底面预热使下压峰值力从1600 N降至接近1100 N，并使峰值温度从未预热时的70 °C升至约100 °C，从而减缓厚度方向的温度梯度，并使轴向力分布更加稳定。同时，利用实验数据训练了两个多层感知机人工神经网络（ANN），以预测温度和轴向力随时间的响应，R2介于0.91与0.99之间，表明其在所测试的FSW参数空间内具有较高的准确性和较强的泛化能力。CEL计算模拟准确预测了焊接区域形貌，包括搅拌区、热机械影响区和热影响区；而CEL–ANN联合框架能够快速生成工艺图，标示出存在缺陷和无缺陷的焊接工艺窗口。获得的最优条件为1500 rpm、30 mm/min及60 °C预热；在1000 rpm且未预热的条件下，则因虫洞缺陷形成而表现出较差的焊接性。这些发现为制备可靠的HDPE接头提供了以工艺为导向的设计指南，并展示了计算建模与机器学习的集成如何实现高效优化，为理解热塑性材料FSW过程的关键特征提供了有价值的见解，并支持其在工业应用中的更广泛采用。

Integration of DMAIC and Two-Level Fuzzy AHP for Diagnosing and Prioritizing Waste and Potential Causes of Welding Defects in a Sidebeam Bogie Frame集成DMAIC与两级模糊AHP，诊断转向架侧梁构架焊接中的浪费及焊接缺陷潜在原因并确定其优先级

I Putu Dhanu Artha, Anak Agung Istri Agung Sri Komaladewi, I Gusti Ngurah Priambadi

Welding defects remain a critical quality issue in the robotic minor assembly welding process of the TB1014 Sidebeam Bogie Frame, affecting production efficiency and product quality. This study aims to evaluate baseline process performance, identify and prioritize manufacturing waste and its underlying causes, and propose a structured improvement roadmap. A Lean Six Sigma framework using the Define–Measure–Analyze–Improve–Control (DMAIC) approach was integrated with a two-level Fuzzy Analytic Hierarchy Process (Fuzzy AHP). Process performance was evaluated using Value Stream Mapping (VSM), time study, a u′-control chart, Defects Per Million Opportunities (DPMO), and sigma level analysis. We then applied Fuzzy AHP to expert judgments from ten respondents to prioritize four waste categories, followed by a 6M fishbone analysis and a second Fuzzy AHP assessment of potential cause categories. The results identified 485 welding defects across 21 inspected units, corresponding to a DPMO of 136,658.21 and a sigma level of 2.60. Undercut accounted for 57.1% of defects, while Process Cycle Efficiency (PCE) was only 4.88%. Defects emerged as the dominant waste category (0.7174), while Machine (0.4803), Method (0.2890), and Man (0.1596) were the leading potential cause categories. However, weak expert agreement and sensitivity to respondent selection indicate uncertainty in the prioritization results. Based on these findings and corrective-action records, an improvement roadmap is proposed focusing on seam-tracking verification, robot parameter standardization, jig and fixture positioning, tack welding, and fit-up control. Microstructure and surface performance of detonation gun sprayed 80Ni20Cr and NiCrAlY coatings on Al 6061-T6 substrate for structural applications面向结构应用的 Al 6061-T6 基体上爆炸喷涂 80Ni20Cr 和 NiCrAlY 涂层的微观组织与表面性能

T. Sampath kumar, Rajaram Ganesan, M.M. Reddy

Two protective coatings, 80Ni20Cr and NiCrAlY, were deposited on Al 6061-T6 alloy substrates using the Detonation Gun (D-Gun) thermal spray process. The deposited coatings were studied for their microstructure, phase composition, surface roughness, hardness, corrosion behaviour, wettability, and solid particle erosion resistance. Characterisation was done using SEM-EDS, XRD, AFM, nanoindentation, potentiodynamic polarisation, contact angle measurement, and erosion testing. SEM-EDS results showed that both coatings bonded well to the substrate with uniform thickness. The NiCrAlY coating had a denser microstructure with lower porosity compared to 80Ni20Cr. XRD identified CrNi phases with FCC crystal structure in 80Ni20Cr and Al₂NiY phases with orthorhombic structure in NiCrAlY. Nanocrystalline grain structure was observed in both coatings. AFM measurements showed that NiCrAlY had the lowest surface roughness among all three samples. Nanoindentation results showed that NiCrAlY hardness is approximately 10 times higher than 80Ni20Cr. Specifically, NiCrAlY showed a hardness of 7895.36 MPa (731.2 HV), compared with 780.11 MPa (72.25 HV) for 80Ni20Cr and 708.68 MPa for the uncoated Al 6061 substrate. Potentiodynamic polarisation tests in 3.5 wt% NaCl solution confirmed that NiCrAlY recorded the lowest corrosion current density of 6.625 × 10 -7 A/cm² and a corrosion rate of 0.00567 mmpy. The 80Ni20Cr coating gave a corrosion current density of 1.585 × 10 - 6 A/cm² and a corrosion rate of 0.01427 mmpy. Both figures are considerably lower than the bare substrate at 0.5185 mmpy. Contact angle measurements confirmed superhydrophobic behaviour for NiCrAlY and near-superhydrophobic behaviour for 80Ni20Cr. Solid particle erosion test results showed that 80Ni20Cr recorded the lowest mass loss. Under the laboratory conditions used (3.5 wt% NaCl at room temperature; a single erosion condition of 90° impact angle, 40 m/s and 5 min), NiCrAlY showed the better hardness and corrosion resistance, whereas 80Ni20Cr showed the better erosion resistance. The surface properties of metal-ceramic coatings based on titanium dioxide are described in dependence on the detonation spraying conditions. It is found that such properties as surface roughness, surface thickness and its hydrophobicity can be controlled in the production process by selecting certain values of the technological parameters of the spraying process.
