Effect of Laser Remelting on the Microstructure, Mechanical and Tribological Properties of Detonation-Sprayed NiCrFeBSiC Coatings for Mechanical Engineering Applications激光重熔对机械工程用爆炸喷涂NiCrFeBSiC涂层微观组织、力学性能及摩擦学性能的影响

Dastan Buitkenov, Nurmakhanbet Raisov, Laila Sulyubayeva, A.Y. Zhakupova, Daryn Baizhan, Nurbol Berdimuratov

This study investigates the effect of laser remelting on the microstructure, phase composition, mechanical properties, tribological behavior, and corrosion resistance of self-fluxing NiCrFeBSiC coatings deposited onto AISI 321 stainless steel by detonation spraying. The coatings were characterized before and after laser treatment using scanning electron microscopy (SEM), energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS), X-ray diffraction (XRD), instrumented indentation, adhesion testing, ball-on-disk tribometry, and potentiodynamic polarization measurements. SEM analysis revealed that laser remelting reduced coating porosity from 0.306 ± 0.014% to 0.15 ± 0.09%, accompanied by microstructural densification and a reduction in coating thickness. XRD analysis confirmed the preservation of the principal crystalline phases, including Ni3Fe, Cr7C3, CrB, and Ni3B, following laser treatment. The Vickers hardness increased from 1049.1 ± 43.4 to 1206.1 ± 63.1 HV, while the elastic modulus increased from 233.6 ± 6.2 to 258.4 ± 5.3 GPa. Furthermore, adhesion strength increased by 19.4%, whereas surface roughness decreased by 19.2%. Laser remelting significantly improved tribological performance, reducing the average coefficient of friction from 0.535 ± 0.048 to 0.409 ± 0.086 and the specific wear rate from 2.864 × 10−4 to 1.447 × 10−4 mm3/(N·m), corresponding to a 49.5% reduction in wear. Electrochemical measurements indicated a modest decrease in corrosion current density of approximately 3.0%, accompanied by changes in the morphology of corrosion-affected regions. The improvements are associated with laser-induced microstructural densification, redistribution of alloying elements, and preservation of hard reinforcing phases. These findings demonstrate the potential of combining detonation spraying with laser remelting to produce wear-resistant nickel-based protective coatings for mechanical and power engineering applications.本研究考察了激光重熔对通过爆炸喷涂沉积于AISI 321不锈钢上的自熔性NiCrFeBSiC涂层的微观组织、相组成、力学性能、摩擦学行为及耐腐蚀性的影响。采用扫描电子显微镜（SEM）、能量色散X射线光谱（EDS）、X射线衍射（XRD）、仪器化压痕、结合强度测试、球-盘摩擦试验及动电位极化测量，对激光处理前后的涂层进行了表征。SEM分析表明，激光重熔使涂层孔隙率由0.306 ± 0.014%降至0.15 ± 0.09%，同时伴随微观组织致密化和涂层厚度减小。XRD分析证实，激光处理后，Ni3Fe、Cr7C3、CrB和Ni3B等主要晶相得以保留。维氏硬度由1049.1 ± 43.4 HV提高至1206.1 ± 63.1 HV，弹性模量由233.6 ± 6.2 GPa提高至258.4 ± 5.3 GPa。此外，结合强度提高了19.4%，表面粗糙度降低了19.2%。激光重熔显著改善了摩擦学性能，使平均摩擦系数由0.535 ± 0.048降至0.409 ± 0.086，比磨损率由2.864 × 10−4 mm3/(N·m)降至1.447 × 10−4 mm3/(N·m)，相当于磨损降低了49.5%。电化学测量表明，腐蚀电流密度小幅降低约3.0%，同时腐蚀影响区域的形貌发生变化。这些性能改善与激光诱导的微观组织致密化、合金元素再分布及硬质增强相的保留有关。研究结果表明，将爆炸喷涂与激光重熔相结合，在制备适用于机械与动力工程的耐磨镍基防护涂层方面具有潜力。

Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于高密度聚乙烯搅拌摩擦焊焊接质量预测的计算建模与机器学习建模方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. Microstructure and surface performance of detonation gun sprayed 80Ni20Cr and NiCrAlY coatings on Al 6061-T6 substrate for structural applications用于结构应用的 Al 6061-T6 基体上爆炸喷涂 80Ni20Cr 和 NiCrAlY 涂层的微观组织与表面性能

T. Sampath kumar, Rajaram Ganesan, M.M. Reddy

Two protective coatings, 80Ni20Cr and NiCrAlY, were deposited on Al 6061-T6 alloy substrates using the Detonation Gun (D-Gun) thermal spray process. The deposited coatings were studied for their microstructure, phase composition, surface roughness, hardness, corrosion behaviour, wettability, and solid particle erosion resistance. Characterisation was done using SEM-EDS, XRD, AFM, nanoindentation, potentiodynamic polarisation, contact angle measurement, and erosion testing. SEM-EDS results showed that both coatings bonded well to the substrate with uniform thickness. The NiCrAlY coating had a denser microstructure with lower porosity compared to 80Ni20Cr. XRD identified CrNi phases with FCC crystal structure in 80Ni20Cr and Al₂NiY phases with orthorhombic structure in NiCrAlY. Nanocrystalline grain structure was observed in both coatings. AFM measurements showed that NiCrAlY had the lowest surface roughness among all three samples. Nanoindentation results showed that NiCrAlY hardness is approximately 10 times higher than 80Ni20Cr. Specifically, NiCrAlY showed a hardness of 7895.36 MPa (731.2 HV), compared with 780.11 MPa (72.25 HV) for 80Ni20Cr and 708.68 MPa for the uncoated Al 6061 substrate. Potentiodynamic polarisation tests in 3.5 wt% NaCl solution confirmed that NiCrAlY recorded the lowest corrosion current density of 6.625 × 10 -7 A/cm² and a corrosion rate of 0.00567 mmpy. The 80Ni20Cr coating gave a corrosion current density of 1.585 × 10 - 6 A/cm² and a corrosion rate of 0.01427 mmpy. Both figures are considerably lower than the bare substrate at 0.5185 mmpy. Contact angle measurements confirmed superhydrophobic behaviour for NiCrAlY and near-superhydrophobic behaviour for 80Ni20Cr. Solid particle erosion test results showed that 80Ni20Cr recorded the lowest mass loss. Under the laboratory conditions used (3.5 wt% NaCl at room temperature; a single erosion condition of 90° impact angle, 40 m/s and 5 min), NiCrAlY showed the better hardness and corrosion resistance, whereas 80Ni20Cr showed the better erosion resistance.
