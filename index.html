The Effect of Auxiliary Electrical Energy Engineering Friction Stir Welding Technology (EAFSW): A Review辅助电能工程摩擦搅拌焊接技术（EAFSW）的影响：综述

Abdulhak Sami, Alaa Daham Younis

Friction Stir Welding (FSW) has been recognized as a reliable and versatile solid-state joining process capable of providing high-quality joints without any melt-related distortions. Despite the above-mentioned advantages, traditional FSW has certain drawbacks related to the large values of process forces and torque, the fast wear of the tool, and the limited ability to be used with high-strength metals and hard-to-weld materials (steels, titanium alloys). Various modifications of FSW utilizing auxiliary energy sources have been proposed for overcoming these deficiencies, but Electrically Assisted Friction Stir Welding (EAFSW) has gained much popularity during the last twenty years [1], [12]. In EAFSW, an electric current is fed into the rotating tool and/or the work piece, thus creating the localized Joule heat and sometimes a thermal electro plastic effect resulting in the softening of material ahead and around the tool and reducing the flow stresses of the welding materials. Consequently, the application of an electric current allows lowering the axial force, traverse force, and torque, extending the life of the tool, increasing the speed of welding, and expanding the spectrum of materials that can be welded together [2],[5]. The current paper reviews the results obtained in the field of EAFSW and focuses on the physical principles and mechanisms behind EAFSW, electrode design and circuit configuration, effects of electrical parameters on forces and torque, microstructure and mechanical properties of aluminum, magnesium, titanium, steel and dissimilar materials and composites joints made using EAFSW, numerical simulations and comparisons with other auxiliary-energy-assisted FSW processes. Finally, principal problems associated with current distribution, electrode wear, arcing, safety and cost factors, as well as the necessity of electro-thermo-mechanical modeling will be highlighted in this review.摩擦搅拌焊接（FSW）已被公认为一种可靠且用途广泛的固相连接工艺，能够在不产生任何与熔化相关变形的情况下提供高质量接头。尽管具有上述优点，传统 FSW 仍存在一些缺点，主要与较大的工艺力和扭矩、工具的快速磨损，以及在高强度金属和难焊材料（钢、钛合金）上的适用性有限有关。为克服这些不足，人们提出了多种利用辅助能量源的 FSW 改进方法，但在过去二十年中，电辅助摩擦搅拌焊接（EAFSW）获得了广泛关注 [1], [12]。在 EAFSW 中，电流被输入旋转工具和/或工件，从而产生局部焦耳热，并有时产生热电塑效应，使工具前方及周围材料软化并降低焊接材料的流动应力。因此，施加电流可降低轴向力、横向力和扭矩，延长工具寿命，提高焊接速度，并扩大可焊接材料的范围 [2],[5]。本文综述了 EAFSW 领域已获得的研究结果，重点讨论 EAFSW 背后的物理原理与机理、电极设计与电路配置、电参数对力和扭矩的影响、采用 EAFSW 制备的铝、镁、钛、钢、异种材料及复合材料接头的显微组织和力学性能、数值模拟，以及与其他辅助能量辅助 FSW 工艺的比较。最后，本文还将强调与电流分布、电极磨损、电弧、安全和成本因素相关的主要问题，以及开展电-热-机械建模的必要性。

Recent developments and current status of keyhole TIG welding: A comprehensive review小孔钨极惰性气体焊接的最新进展与现状：综合综述

Haripraneeth Emayavarambamoorthy, Senthil Ponnusamy, Chidanand Magadum

Gas tungsten arc welding is a widely used metal joining process across manufacturing industries due to its ability to produce high- quality welds with excellent precision and cleanliness. However, gas tungsten arc welding is limited by relatively low welding speeds, reduced filler material deposition rates, and shallow penetration, which constrain its efficiency for medium and thick section fabrication. Keyhole tungsten inert gas welding extends the capabilities of conventional gas tungsten arc welding by enabling stable, single-pass, full-penetration welding in medium to thick section materials. Although welding speeds in true keyhole mode are governed by weld pool stability, overall productivity and weld quality are improved through the elimination of complex edge preparation and multi-pass welding. Compared to high-power-density processes such as plasma arc welding, laser beam welding, and electron beam welding, keyhole tungsten inert gas welding provides a cost-effective and operationally simpler alternative for medium to thick section fabrication. This review examines the current research status and technological progress of keyhole tungsten inert gas welding, classifying and summarizing the process based on microstructural evolution, artificial intelligence-based process monitoring, multiphysics numerical modeling, and emerging technology variants. By comprehensively analyzing the process characteristics and underlying mechanisms, the novelty of this work lies in integrating these aspects into a unified framework, thereby strengthening the knowledge base of keyhole tungsten inert gas welding and providing clear direction for future research. Furthermore, this review offers a valuable reference for researchers and industry practitioners seeking to advance high-productivity welding technologies.由于能够获得高质量焊缝并具有优异的精度和洁净度，气体钨弧焊是一种广泛应用于制造业的金属连接工艺。然而，气体钨弧焊受限于相对较低的焊接速度、较低的填充材料沉积率以及较浅的熔深，这些因素制约了其在中厚板制造中的效率。小孔钨极惰性气体焊接通过实现稳定的单道全熔透焊接，扩展了传统气体钨弧焊在中厚板材料中的能力。尽管真正小孔模式下的焊接速度受焊池稳定性支配，但通过取消复杂的坡口准备和多道焊接，整体生产率和焊接质量得到了提高。与等离子弧焊、激光束焊和电子束焊等高功率密度工艺相比，小孔钨极惰性气体焊接为中厚板制造提供了一种更具成本效益且操作更简便的替代方案。本综述考察了小孔钨极惰性气体焊接的当前研究状态和技术进展，并依据显微组织演化、基于人工智能的过程监测、多物理场数值建模以及新兴技术变体对该工艺进行了分类和总结。通过对工艺特征及其内在机理的综合分析，本文的创新之处在于将这些方面整合到统一框架中，从而强化了对小孔钨极惰性气体焊接的知识基础，并为未来研究提供了清晰方向。此外，本综述还为希望推动高生产率焊接技术发展的研究人员和工业从业者提供了有价值的参考。

Depositing various coatings on surface of engineering components with the aim to improve their performance concerning wear, corrosion, friction and thermal protection is already a standard practice. Depositing metallic NiTi shape memory alloy coatings may be a viable alternative for hard ceramic coatings. NiTi coatings offer additional benefits originating from unique functional thermomechanical properties. However, fabrication of thick NiTi coatings turned out to be difficult. Standard electroplating and laser cladding methods are not suitable for NiTi the most widely used plasma spray methods tend to produce chemically inhomogeneous coatings that do not transform martensitically, cold sprayed NiTi coatings suffer from poor adhesion to the substrates. In this work we report on first ever successful fabrication of thick NiTi coatings (100-300 um) that display functional thermomechanical properties and simultaneously show very good adherence to the substrate. We used high velocity air fuel thermal spray method to fabricate NiTi coatings deposited on mild steel using four different sets of processing parameters. Chemical composition, porosity, microstructure, phase transformation and functional thermomechanical properties of the NiTi coatings were evaluated. Although the coatings contain inhomogeneous microstructure, voids, oxide particles, high density of dislocation defects and internal stress, they undergo martensitic transformation upon cooling and or mechanical loading.
