Exploration of friction stir welding butted thin sheet NiTi with AA6061, AA2024, and AA7075NiTi薄板与AA6061、AA2024和AA7075搅拌摩擦对接焊的探索

Brayden Terry, A. Strauss

Friction stir welding was investigated as a method for producing dissimilar butt joints of 1-mm-thick NiTi sheet with three aluminum alloys: AA6061-T6, AA2024-T3, and AA7075-T6. Dissimilar welding of these materials could enable structural integration of shape memory alloys with common aerospace aluminum alloys, but differences in thermal and mechanical behavior make joining difficult. Joining NiTi and AA6061 was successful, while NiTi and AA2024 showed mixed results under tested conditions. NiTi and AA7075 did not join under tested conditions. Difficulty in joining AA2024 and AA7075 came from post-weld cracking attributed to residual stresses and thermal strain due to mismatch in coefficients of thermal expansion combined with lower ductility in the higher-strength aluminum alloys. Tensile testing showed that AA6061–NiTi welds retained moderate strength, with an average ultimate tensile strength of 167.8 MPa and a weld efficiency of 57.9% relative to AA6061-T6, while AA2024–NiTi welds showed lower strength and ductility, with an average tensile strength of 120 MPa and a weld efficiency of 27.6%. Fracture behavior suggests that failure was controlled by the ability of the interface to bear load across the joint. Microscopy revealed a region of dispersed NiTi particles in both AA6061 and AA2024 stir zones. Larger particles showed evidence of aluminum diffusion, while smaller particles were enriched in either Ni or Ti, consistent with formation of Ni-Al and Ti-Al intermetallic compounds. No intermetallic layer was observed on the micron scale at the weld interfaces for either AA6061 or AA2024. These results demonstrate that FSW can produce dissimilar Al alloy–NiTi joints, but mechanical performance is variable with alloy selection.本研究探索了采用搅拌摩擦焊将1 mm厚NiTi薄板分别与三种铝合金AA6061-T6、AA2024-T3和AA7075-T6制成异种材料对接接头。这些材料的异种焊接可实现形状记忆合金与常用航空航天铝合金的结构集成，但其热学和力学行为的差异使连接面临困难。NiTi与AA6061成功实现了连接，而NiTi与AA2024在所测试条件下的连接结果不一。NiTi与AA7075在所测试条件下未能实现连接。AA2024和AA7075的连接困难源于焊后开裂，这被归因于残余应力、热膨胀系数不匹配引起的热应变，以及较高强度铝合金较低延性的共同作用。拉伸试验表明，AA6061–NiTi焊接接头保持了中等强度，平均极限抗拉强度为167.8 MPa，相对于AA6061-T6的焊接接头效率为57.9%；而AA2024–NiTi焊接接头的强度和延性较低，平均抗拉强度为120 MPa，焊接接头效率为27.6%。断裂行为表明，失效受界面跨接头传递载荷的能力控制。显微观察显示，AA6061和AA2024的搅拌区内均存在NiTi颗粒弥散分布的区域。较大颗粒显示出铝扩散的迹象，而较小颗粒则富集Ni或Ti，这与Ni-Al和Ti-Al金属间化合物的形成相一致。在AA6061和AA2024的焊接界面处，均未在微米尺度上观察到金属间化合物层。这些结果表明，搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）能够制备铝合金–NiTi异种材料接头，但其力学性能随合金选择而变化。

Yingying Zhang, Lingyu Sheng, Shanshan Xu, Jian Zhang, Junhao XU, Chen Xu

ABSTRACT Weld seams in membrane structures exhibit a significantly higher failure probability than the base material and often act as initiation points for structural damage. Their mechanical properties are strongly influenced by welding process parameters, including temperature, time, and pressure. This work investigated the effects of welding parameters on the mechanical performance of Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC)- and Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE)-coated fabric weld seams through orthogonal experiments, analyzing the failure modes, mechanical characteristics, and microstructural variations of three weld configurations to reveal the failure mechanism and establish a high-accuracy strength prediction model. Joint efficiency is defined as the ratio of the ultimate bearing capacity of welded joints to that of the base material. For PVC-coated fabrics, the lap weld seams reached a joint efficiency of 108.23 %, with 78 % of specimens failing in the base material. The joint efficiency of single-covered and double-covered butt weld seams dropped to a minimum of 78.54 %, attributed to stress concentration and bending effects. For PTFE-coated fabrics, the maximum joint efficiency reached 101.57 %. The error of the established strength prediction model is less than or equal to 5 %. Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene高密度聚乙烯搅拌摩擦焊焊接质量预测的计算建模与机器学习方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. Compared to the unreinforced coating, microstructure, phase composition, bond strength, abrasive wear, coefficient of friction, and microhardness were assessed. Characterisation was done using SEM-EDS, XRD, AFM, nanoindentation, potentiodynamic polarisation, contact angle measurement, and erosion testing. SEM-EDS results showed that both coatings bonded well to the substrate with uniform thickness. The NiCrAlY coating had a denser microstructure with lower porosity compared to 80Ni20Cr. XRD identified CrNi phases with FCC crystal structure in 80Ni20Cr and Al₂NiY phases with orthorhombic structure in NiCrAlY. Nanocrystalline grain structure was observed in both coatings. AFM measurements showed that NiCrAlY had the lowest surface roughness among all three samples. Nanoindentation results showed that NiCrAlY hardness is approximately 10 times higher than 80Ni20Cr. Specifically, NiCrAlY showed a hardness of 7895.36 MPa (731.2 HV), compared with 780.11 MPa (72.25 HV) for 80Ni20Cr and 708.68 MPa for the uncoated Al 6061 substrate.
