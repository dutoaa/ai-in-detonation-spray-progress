This study compares the microstructure and corrosion behavior of LPBF A20X under four material conditions: base material (BM), as-welded stir zone (SZ), solution-treated and peak-aged base material (BM–PA), and solution-treated and peak-aged stir zone (SZ–PA). Ignatius Echezona Ekengwu, Bonaventure Onyeka Ekengwu, Ikechukwu Okoli, Obiora Clement Okafor

Weld groove geometry and post-weld cooling regime are among the least systematically investigated factors influencing the performance of arc-welded joints, and even when they have been studied, the analysis has almost always been limited to a single optimisation criterion. This paper re-examines shielded metal arc welding of AISI/SAE 1020 mild steel across a 55–60° single-V groove range and three quenching media (air, water, palm oil), using six mechanical responses, and advances the analysis in four directions. First, a normalised Type III effect-share analysis establishes the practical rather than merely statistical weight of each factor: the quenching medium accounts for 70.3–94.6% of the estimated effect across the six responses, while groove angle accounts for 4.4–25.7%, and the factor interaction never exceeds 3.2%. Synthesis of Amorphous and Crystalline Silicon Dioxide Powder Composites from Rice Husk Ash for Thermal Spray Deposit利用稻壳灰制备用于热喷涂沉积的无定形和晶体二氧化硅粉末复合材料

Daryn Baizhan, Temirlan Alimbekuly, Akbar S. Alibekov, Elzhan Birzhan, G.S. Bektasova, Zhuldyz B. Sagdoldina

This paper presents the synthesis of silicon dioxide (SiO₂) from rice husk ash through chemical treatment followed by heat treatment at 800 °C for 3 h. The structural and physicochemical properties of the synthesized silica were investigated using X-ray diffraction (XRD), Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR), and laser diffraction particle size analysis. The synthesized silica exhibited an amorphous structure, with a median particle size (D50) of approximately 88.9 μm. Subsequent annealing at 1200 °C led to crystallization of the silica, resulting in the formation of quartz and cristobalite phases. FTIR analysis revealed characteristic absorption bands associated with Si–O–Si stretching vibrations in the 1000–1100 cm⁻¹ region, as well as bands attributed to hydroxyl groups. The obtained crystalline silica materials demonstrate potential for use as powder materials in thermal spraying technologies.本文提出通过化学处理并随后在800 °C下热处理3 h，从稻壳灰中合成二氧化硅（SiO₂）。采用X射线衍射（XRD）、傅里叶变换红外光谱（FTIR）和激光衍射粒度分析研究了所合成二氧化硅的结构和理化性质。所合成的二氧化硅呈无定形结构，中位粒径（D50）约为88.9 μm。随后在1200 °C下退火使二氧化硅发生晶化，形成石英和方石英相。FTIR分析显示，在1000–1100 cm⁻¹区域存在与Si–O–Si伸缩振动相关的特征吸收带，并且还观察到归属于羟基的吸收带。所得晶态二氧化硅材料显示出作为热喷涂技术中粉末材料的应用潜力。

Arystanbek Kussainov, Indira Abizhanova, M.K. Kaliaskarova, Yermakhan Molbossynov, Нуртолеу Магазов

The hardness and post-scratch surface morphology of Cr₃C₂–NiCr coatings subjected to ultrasonic nanocrystal surface modification (UNSM) were examined for detonation-sprayed (DS) and high-velocity oxy-fuel (HVOF) deposits. Four treated samples per deposition route were evaluated at static loads of 20 and 30 N and vibration amplitudes of 20 and 30 µm. Instrumental hardness was measured at a load of 300 mN with a dwell time of 10 s. The DS samples exhibited hardness values of 820–970 HV, whereas the HVOF samples covered the wider range of 546–1165 HV. The highest values were recorded for DS-T1 at 20 N/20 µm and HVOF-T4 at 30 N/30 µm. SEM fields acquired after scratch testing showed heterogeneous surface contrast and crack-like discontinuities in both coating series. These observations provide qualitative evidence of the post-test surface condition but do not isolate damage caused by UNSM or establish a ranking of scratch resistance. Available mean friction coefficients are reported as supplementary observations for six treated samples. The results demonstrate a sample-dependent hardness response and emphasize the need to assess local mechanical properties together with surface integrity.对经超声纳米晶表面改性（UNSM）的 Cr₃C₂–NiCr 涂层在爆炸喷涂（DS）和高速氧燃料（HVOF）沉积样品上进行了硬度及划痕后表面形貌研究。每种沉积路线各评估了 4 个处理样品，静载荷分别为 20 和 30 N，振动幅值分别为 20 和 30 µm。采用 300 mN 载荷、10 s 保载时间测量仪器硬度。DS 样品的硬度值为 820–970 HV，而 HVOF 样品的范围更宽，为 546–1165 HV。最高值分别记录于 20 N/20 µm 条件下的 DS-T1，以及 30 N/30 µm 条件下的 HVOF-T4。划痕测试后获取的 SEM 图像显示，两类涂层系列均存在非均匀的表面对比和类裂纹不连续特征。这些观察为测试后的表面状态提供了定性证据，但无法将 UNSM 引起的损伤单独区分出来，也不能建立划痕抗力的排序。可获得的平均摩擦系数作为 6 个处理样品的补充观察结果予以报告。结果表明，硬度响应具有样品依赖性，并强调应将局部力学性能与表面完整性结合评估。

A Comparative Analysis of Multitask Neural Networks and Stacking Ensemble Learning for Predicting UTS, Weld Hardness, and HAZ Hardness in Welding Applications用于焊接应用中 UTS、焊缝硬度和 HAZ 硬度预测的多任务神经网络与堆叠集成学习比较分析

Sama Mukhtar, Amit Sata, Gaurang Joshi, Durgesh Srivastava

Accurately predicting welding performance measures like ultimate strength (UTS), weld bead hardness, and HAZ mechanical hardness is crucial for ensuring the structural integrity and performance of welded components. Multitask learning (MTL) refers to a machine learning approach in which one model is designed to handle several interconnected tasks at the same time. Instead of training separate models for each task, MTL shares representations among tasks, allowing them to leverage common patterns while maintaining task-specific distinctions. In this study, we compared two advanced machine learning techniques, namely multitask neural network (MTNN) and stacking ensemble learning, for predicting these parameters based on a shared dataset. A multitask neural network (MTNN) is a specific type of multitask learning (MTL) model that uses a deep neural network architecture to handle multiple related tasks simultaneously. In MTNN, different tasks share some hidden layers while having task-specific output layers. This shared representation allows the model to learn common patterns across tasks while maintaining task-specific outputs. Both methods are evaluated using RMSE and R<sup>2</sup> to determine their predictive accuracy and overall effectiveness. It showed robust prediction strength, as its RMSE outcomes are 0.1288 for UTS, 0.0886 for weld hardness, and 0.1125 for HAZ hardness, whereas R<sup>2</sup> values are 0.6724, 0.9215, and 0.8407, respectively. This underlines that it can generalize well in interrelated tasks. Stacking ensemble learning outperformed MTL in the accuracy of individual tasks: the RMSE for UTS is 0.0263 and R<sup>2</sup> is 0.9863; for weld hardness, it is 0.0467 and 0.9782; and for HAZ hardness, it is 0.1109 and 0.8453. Such results indicate the good ability of ensemble methods to produce highly accurate, task-specific predictions. This comparison reveals the trade-offs between the two approaches. MTL is good in scenarios where the tasks are related and the data are sparse, giving efficient training and good generalization; stacking ensembles work better in the case of accurate, independent predictions. In both cases, they show remarkable potential for improving the predictive power of welding applications, making them suitable precursors to further investigation into hybrid models that bring the best features of both approaches together.准确预测焊接性能指标，如极限强度（UTS）、焊缝硬度和 HAZ 机械硬度，对于确保焊接构件的结构完整性和性能至关重要。多任务学习（MTL）是一种机器学习方法，其中一个模型被设计用于同时处理多个相互关联的任务。与针对每个任务分别训练独立模型不同，MTL 在任务之间共享表示，使其能够利用共同模式，同时保留任务特定差异。在本研究中，我们基于一个共享数据集，对两种先进的机器学习技术，即多任务神经网络（MTNN）和堆叠集成学习，进行了比较，用于预测这些参数。多任务神经网络（MTNN）是一种特定类型的多任务学习（MTL）模型，它使用深度神经网络架构同时处理多个相关任务。在 MTNN 中，不同任务共享部分隐藏层，同时具有任务特定的输出层。这种共享表示使模型能够学习跨任务的共同模式，同时保持任务特定输出。两种方法均使用 RMSE 和 R<sup>2</sup> 进行评估，以确定其预测准确性和整体有效性。结果表明，该方法具有较强的预测能力，其 RMSE 分别为 UTS 0.1288、焊缝硬度 0.0886 和 HAZ 硬度 0.1125，而 R<sup>2</sup> 值分别为 0.6724、0.9215 和 0.8407。这表明其能够在相关任务中实现良好的泛化能力。堆叠集成学习在单个任务的准确性方面优于 MTL：UTS 的 RMSE 为 0.0263，R<sup>2</sup> 为 0.9863；焊缝硬度的 RMSE 为 0.0467，R<sup>2</sup> 为 0.9782；HAZ 硬度的 RMSE 为 0.1109，R<sup>2</sup> 为 0.8453。这些结果表明，集成方法在生成高精度、任务特定预测方面具有良好能力。该比较揭示了两种方法之间的权衡。MTL 适用于任务相关且数据稀疏的场景，可提供高效训练和良好泛化；而堆叠集成在需要高精度、独立预测的情况下表现更好。两种方法都显示出提高焊接应用预测能力的显著潜力，因此可作为进一步研究混合模型的合适前导，后者可将两种方法的最佳特征结合起来。
