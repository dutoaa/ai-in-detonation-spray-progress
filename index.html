Development and optimization of parameter recommendation model for automotive resistance spot welding汽车电阻点焊参数推荐模型的开发与优化

Yongbing Li, YunFan Zhou, YouRong Zhang, JianNan Zhang, YongQuan Jia, YongBing Li

The efficiency and reliability of welding parameter design directly affect weld quality, process stability, and manufacturing responsiveness in automotive manufacturing. Traditional parameter design, heavily reliant on empirical trials and extensive testing, is time-consuming and ill-suited for the growing variety of sheet-stack combinations in modern vehicles. This study proposes an intelligent agent model for parameter generation in Resistance spot welding (RSW). A heterogeneous ensemble learning model to map sheet attributes and target nugget size to welding parameters is established as the base model. On this basis, a novel physics-informed constraint—the heating intensity—which characterizes the heat input path is introduced to address the limitation of purely data-driven base model, particularly its lack of uniqueness and poor generalization. This variable is incorporated into the training process via a multi-loss optimization scheme that simultaneously minimizes parameter prediction error and heating intensity deviation. Results demonstrate that the proposed framework significantly improves prediction accuracy under both same-vehicle and cross-vehicle conditions. Results showed that the heating intensity constraint effectively compresses the solution space, ensuring the uniqueness and physical consistency of generated parameters. By adjusting the heating intensity, the model can controllably generate strong-regime and weak-regime parameter combinations, thereby enhancing the engineering applicability and interpretability of the ensemble learning approach for automotive RSW parameter design. This study paves the way for intelligent management of the entire design and manufacturing of RSW process.焊接参数设计的效率与可靠性直接影响汽车制造中的焊接质量、工艺稳定性和制造响应能力。传统参数设计高度依赖经验性试验和大量测试，耗时较长，难以适应现代汽车中日益多样化的板材叠层组合。本研究提出了一种用于电阻点焊（RSW）参数生成的智能体模型。首先，建立异构集成学习模型作为基础模型，将板材属性和目标熔核尺寸映射为焊接参数。在此基础上，引入一种表征热输入路径的新型物理信息约束——加热强度，以解决纯数据驱动基础模型的局限性，尤其是其解不唯一和泛化能力不足的问题。通过多损失优化方案将该变量纳入训练过程，同时最小化参数预测误差和加热强度偏差。结果表明，所提出的框架在同车型和跨车型条件下均显著提高了预测精度。结果显示，加热强度约束能够有效压缩解空间，确保生成参数的唯一性和物理一致性。通过调节加热强度，模型能够可控地生成强规范和弱规范参数组合，从而增强集成学习方法在汽车RSW参数设计中的工程适用性与可解释性。本研究为RSW工艺设计与制造全流程的智能化管理奠定了基础。

Multi-objective optimization of pulsed tungsten inert gas welding process parameters for Ti-6Al-4 V alloy using response surface methodology, Rao-3 algorithm, and TOPSIS采用响应面法、Rao-3算法和TOPSIS对Ti-6Al-4 V合金脉冲钨极惰性气体保护焊工艺参数进行多目标优化

Kamlesh Kumar, Manish Sharma, Tijo D, Guniputi Bala Narasimha, Abhishek Kumar Pandey, Hargovind Soni

Pulsed tungsten inert gas (TIG) welding is a variant of the TIG welding process and is widely employed in various industries for joining titanium alloys. Owing to its precise control over heat input and periodic variation in arc energy, pulsed TIG welding offers greater flexibility in controlling weld bead geometry. In the present study, 20 bead-on-plate weld experiments were conducted using Pulsed tungsten inert gas (TIG) welding process by varying its process parameters i.e. peak currents ranging from 100 to 160 A and pulse frequencies ranging from 1 to 50 Hz. From 20 bead-on-plate welds, second-order response surface models were developed for bead width, penetration depth and HAZ width, which were then optimized using the parameter-free multi-objective Rao-3 algorithm. Furthermore, TOPSIS was employed to select the best compromise solution from each Pareto front for ten weight sets. The results showed that peak current mainly governed bead width and penetration, whereas pulse frequency controlled the HAZ, and the models predicted the experiments with mean errors below 2.7%. After optimization, the minimum bead width and HAZ width reached 6.67 mm and 1.05 mm, which are 11% and 15% lower than the smallest measured values, respectively. Moreover, a pulse frequency of 28–33 Hz was selected under all weights, and full penetration was achieved at 110–121 A with bead and HAZ widths 28–35% and 17–33% smaller than those of the fully penetrated welds at 160 A. Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于高密度聚乙烯搅拌摩擦焊焊接质量预测的计算建模与机器学习建模方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. These findings provide process-driven design guidelines for developing robust HDPE joints and demonstrate how integrating computational modeling with machine learning enables efficient optimization, offering valuable insights into the key characteristics of the FSW process in thermoplastics and supporting its broader adoption in industrial applications.搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）已被公认为连接热塑性材料的一种有效技术。本研究结合计算方法与机器学习方法，对高密度聚乙烯（HDPE）搅拌摩擦焊中的焊接温度、垂直轴向力、材料流动及缺陷形成等特征进行全面的预测研究，并通过实验结果加以验证。研究建立了三维耦合欧拉–拉格朗日（CEL）数值模型，以模拟不同转速、焊接速度及底部预热条件下的HDPE焊接过程。通过实验数据验证了CEL数值模型，结果表明，该模型在预测孔洞形成以及热响应和力学响应方面与实验结果高度吻合。结果显示，底面预热可有效减小温度梯度并稳定垂直力波动。定量而言，60 °C底面预热使峰值下压力由1600 N降至接近1100 N，并使峰值温度由未预热时的70 °C升至约100 °C，从而减小厚度方向的温度梯度并稳定轴向力分布。同时，利用实验数据训练了两个多层感知机人工神经网络（ANN），以预测温度和轴向力的时变响应，其R2介于0.91至0.99之间，表明其在所测试的FSW参数空间内具有较高的准确性和较强的泛化能力。CEL计算模拟准确预测了焊接区域的形貌，包括搅拌区、热机械影响区和热影响区；而CEL–ANN组合框架能够快速生成工艺图，明确有缺陷和无缺陷的焊接工艺窗口。在所测试条件下，最优工艺条件为1500 rpm、30 mm/min及60 °C预热；在1000 rpm且不预热时，由于虫洞缺陷的形成，观察到较差的可焊性。这些发现为制备性能可靠的HDPE接头提供了以工艺为导向的设计指南，并展示了计算建模与机器学习的融合如何实现高效优化，为深入理解热塑性塑料FSW工艺的关键特征提供了有价值的见解，同时支持该技术在工业应用中的更广泛采用。

Microstructure and surface performance of detonation gun sprayed 80Ni20Cr and NiCrAlY coatings on Al 6061-T6 substrate for structural applications面向结构应用的 Al 6061-T6 基体上爆炸喷涂 80Ni20Cr 和 NiCrAlY 涂层的微观组织与表面性能

T. Sampath kumar, Rajaram Ganesan, M.M. Reddy

Two protective coatings, 80Ni20Cr and NiCrAlY, were deposited on Al 6061-T6 alloy substrates using the Detonation Gun (D-Gun) thermal spray process. The deposited coatings were studied for their microstructure, phase composition, surface roughness, hardness, corrosion behaviour, wettability, and solid particle erosion resistance. Characterisation was done using SEM-EDS, XRD, AFM, nanoindentation, potentiodynamic polarisation, contact angle measurement, and erosion testing. SEM-EDS results showed that both coatings bonded well to the substrate with uniform thickness. The NiCrAlY coating had a denser microstructure with lower porosity compared to 80Ni20Cr. XRD identified CrNi phases with FCC crystal structure in 80Ni20Cr and Al₂NiY phases with orthorhombic structure in NiCrAlY. Nanocrystalline grain structure was observed in both coatings. AFM measurements showed that NiCrAlY had the lowest surface roughness among all three samples. Nanoindentation results showed that NiCrAlY hardness is approximately 10 times higher than 80Ni20Cr. Specifically, NiCrAlY showed a hardness of 7895.36 MPa (731.2 HV), compared with 780.11 MPa (72.25 HV) for 80Ni20Cr and 708.68 MPa for the uncoated Al 6061 substrate. Potentiodynamic polarisation tests in 3.5 wt% NaCl solution confirmed that NiCrAlY recorded the lowest corrosion current density of 6.625 × 10 -7 A/cm² and a corrosion rate of 0.00567 mmpy. The 80Ni20Cr coating gave a corrosion current density of 1.585 × 10 - 6 A/cm² and a corrosion rate of 0.01427 mmpy. Both figures are considerably lower than the bare substrate at 0.5185 mmpy. Contact angle measurements confirmed superhydrophobic behaviour for NiCrAlY and near-superhydrophobic behaviour for 80Ni20Cr. Solid particle erosion test results showed that 80Ni20Cr recorded the lowest mass loss. Under the laboratory conditions used (3.5 wt% NaCl at room temperature; a single erosion condition of 90° impact angle, 40 m/s and 5 min), NiCrAlY showed the better hardness and corrosion resistance, whereas 80Ni20Cr showed the better erosion resistance.
