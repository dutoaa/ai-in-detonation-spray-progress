Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于高密度聚乙烯摩擦搅拌焊焊缝质量预测的计算与机器学习建模方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. These findings provide process-driven design guidelines for developing robust HDPE joints and demonstrate how integrating computational modeling with machine learning enables efficient optimization, offering valuable insights into the key characteristics of the FSW process in thermoplastics and supporting its broader adoption in industrial applications.摩擦搅拌焊（FSW）已被公认为一种连接热塑性材料的有效技术。本研究结合计算方法与机器学习方法，并通过实验结果验证，系统研究了高密度聚乙烯（HDPE）摩擦搅拌焊中的焊接温度、竖直轴向力、材料流动和缺陷形成等FSW特征的预测问题。建立了三维耦合欧拉-拉格朗日（CEL）数值模型，用于模拟在不同工艺参数（转速、焊接速度以及底部预热条件）下的HDPE焊接过程。CEL数值模型与实验数据进行了对比验证，在预测孔洞形成、热响应和力学响应方面表现出明显良好的一致性。结果表明，对底表面进行预热可有效降低热梯度并稳定竖直力波动。定量而言，底表面60 °C预热将峰值压入力从接近1600 N降至约1100 N，并将峰值温度从70 °C（无预热）提高到约100 °C，从而平滑了厚度方向梯度并稳定了轴向力分布。与此同时，基于实验数据训练了两个多层感知器人工神经网络（ANN），用于预测温度和轴向力的时间响应，其R2介于0.91至0.99之间，表明在所测试的FSW参数空间内具有较高精度和较强泛化能力。计算CEL仿真能够准确预测焊缝区形貌，包括搅拌区、热-机械影响区和热影响区；而组合的CEL–ANN框架能够快速生成工艺图，突出显示有缺陷与无缺陷的焊接窗口，其中在1500 rpm、30 mm/min并采用60 °C预热时获得了最佳条件，而在无预热、1000 rpm条件下由于虫洞形成表现出较差的可焊性。这些研究结果为开发稳健的HDPE接头提供了面向工艺的设计指南，并展示了将计算建模与机器学习相结合如何实现高效优化，为理解热塑性材料FSW过程的关键特征提供了有价值的见解，并支持其在工业应用中的更广泛采用。

The automotive industry is adopting the Industry 4.0 model to reduce failures in electrical resistance spot welding by using automated non-destructive testing systems. This study presents a real-time machine vision system that has been implemented on an automotive cabin assembly line to detect weld defects in door frames. The system extracts the physical parameters of each spot weld, including nugget diameter and heat-affected zone, to identify failing robots and welding points and prioritise maintenance actions (maintenance-free, predictive, preventive or corrective). Machine learning models were trained using images from 674 cabins collected over 30 continuous hours. YOLOv8 was used for spot weld detection and feature extraction. A neural network with linear discriminant analysis was applied for defect classification, achieving 95.80% accuracy, 95.61% precision, 96.00% recall and 95.80% F1-score. Additionally, a convolutional neural network was developed for maintenance prediction, achieving 94.72% precision, 95.87% accuracy, 93.97% F1-score and 94.42% recall. Robust Monitoring of Arc Welding Processes: A Generalizable Framework with DVAE and Particle Filter面向电弧焊过程的鲁棒监测：基于 DVAE 与粒子滤波的通用框架

Yue Cao, Hai Lin, YuMing Zhang

Arc welding processes are essential for continuous fabrication but prone to disturbances that impair weld quality, making real-time monitoring critical yet difficult due to complex visual patterns and nonlinear, time-varying dynamics. Deep learning shows promise but faces scalability limits because of its dependence on large labeled datasets and application-specific tuning. We explore whether a unified approach can characterize major arc welding processes across applications and improve scalability through consistent state monitoring. This paper introduces a robust and generalizable monitoring framework for arc welding. It combines unsupervised deep latent representation learning, which extracts compact features from weld pool images, with Bayesian filtering to handle persistent and fluctuating disturbances such as arc radiation and specular reflections. Specifically, a Dynamic Variational Autoencoder (DVAE), consisting of a CNN-based encoder-decoder and an LSTM-based transition model, jointly learns latent representations and their evolution under control inputs. For robust real-time inference, a specialized Particle Filter (PF) propagates the latent and LSTM hidden states, preserving process history while suppressing sensor noise. This design is well suited to welding's slow and inertial dynamics. Validation on GTAW and GMAW without process-specific tuning demonstrates the framework's generalizability and robustness.电弧焊过程是连续制造中的关键工艺，但容易受到扰动影响焊缝质量，因此实时监测至关重要。然而，由于视觉模式复杂且动力学具有非线性、时变特性，实时监测也十分困难。深度学习显示出应用潜力，但由于其依赖大量标注数据且需要针对具体应用进行调参，因而面临可扩展性限制。我们探讨一种统一方法是否能够表征不同应用中的主要电弧焊过程，并通过一致的状态监测提升可扩展性。本文提出一种用于电弧焊的鲁棒且通用的监测框架。该框架将无监督深度潜在表示学习与贝叶斯滤波相结合：前者从熔池图像中提取紧凑特征，后者用于处理诸如弧光辐射和镜面反射等持续性与波动性扰动。具体而言，动态变分自编码器（DVAE）由基于 CNN 的编码器-解码器和基于 LSTM 的转移模型组成，可联合学习潜在表示及其在控制输入作用下的演化。为实现鲁棒的实时推断，专门设计的粒子滤波器（PF）传播潜在状态和 LSTM 隐状态，在抑制传感器噪声的同时保留过程历史。该设计非常适合焊接过程缓慢且具有惯性的动力学特征。对 GTAW 和 GMAW 的验证且无需针对具体工艺调参，表明该框架具有良好的通用性与鲁棒性。

Vu Quang Huy, Vu Minh Thuan, Hoang Van Huong, Tran Minh The Uyen, Van-Thuc Nguyen, Pham Son Minh

Orbital TIG welding is critical in precision manufacturing, where weld defects can affect structural reliability. Manual inspection, analysis, and evaluation of welding defect images are complex because defect characteristics vary in shape, position, and size, making the process time-consuming. This study proposes a method that combines ResNet50 feature extraction with Random Forest classification to improve classification accuracy. A total of 3,219 weld image samples collected from controlled orbital TIG experiments were used for model training and evaluation and divided into four classes: normal weld, lack of fusion, overheating, and uneven weld. Reliable seam segmentation is essential for autonomous robotic welding in construction, where harsh illumination, specular reflections, and thin weld geometries often degrade segmentation performance. This study proposes a reflection-robust seam segmentation framework that enhances a BiSeNetV2 backbone through transfer learning and a hybrid Cross-Entropy--Lovász loss. Rather than increasing architectural complexity, the proposed framework improves reflection robustness through learning-stability-oriented optimization. Experimental results show that the proposed method achieves 81.76\% Joint IoU and 90.73\% mIoU, improving Joint IoU by +22.36 percentage points over the OHEM-based baseline while maintaining identical FLOPs, parameter count, and inference speed. The proposed approach also recovers 96.33\% of severe zero-IoU failure cases under reflective conditions. Comparative experiments across BiSeNetV2, DeepLabV3+, UNet, and SegFormer further demonstrate that the proposed optimization strategy is particularly effective for lightweight real-time segmentation architectures. Qualitative analyses additionally show improved seam continuity and reflection robustness in challenging welding environments.
