Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于高密度聚乙烯搅拌摩擦焊焊接质量预测的计算与机器学习建模方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. These findings provide process-driven design guidelines for developing robust HDPE joints and demonstrate how integrating computational modeling with machine learning enables efficient optimization, offering valuable insights into the key characteristics of the FSW process in thermoplastics and supporting its broader adoption in industrial applications.搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）已被认可为连接热塑性材料的有效技术。本研究综合采用计算与机器学习方法，对高密度聚乙烯（HDPE）搅拌摩擦焊中的焊接温度、竖直轴向力、材料流动和缺陷形成等特征进行预测，并通过实验结果加以验证。研究建立了三维耦合欧拉–拉格朗日（CEL）数值模型，以模拟不同转速、焊接速度及底部预热条件下的HDPE焊接过程。CEL数值模型经实验数据验证，在孔洞形成以及热响应和力学响应的预测方面表现出良好的一致性。结果表明，底面预热能够有效减小温度梯度，并抑制竖直力波动。定量而言，60 °C底面预热使峰值下压力由1600 N降至接近1100 N，并使峰值温度由未预热时的70 °C升至约100 °C，从而减缓厚度方向上的温度梯度，并使轴向力分布更加稳定。同时，利用实验数据训练了两个多层感知机人工神经网络（ANN），以预测温度和轴向力随时间的响应，R2介于0.91与0.99之间，表明其在所测试的FSW参数空间内具有较高的准确性和较强的泛化能力。CEL计算模拟准确预测了焊接区域的形貌，包括搅拌区、热机械影响区和热影响区；而CEL–ANN组合框架则能够快速生成工艺图，标示存在缺陷和无缺陷的焊接工艺窗口。最优条件为转速1500 rpm、焊接速度30 mm/min及60 °C预热；在转速1000 rpm且未预热时，因形成虫洞缺陷而表现出较差的可焊性。这些发现为制备可靠的HDPE接头提供了基于工艺的设计指导，并展示了计算建模与机器学习的集成如何实现高效优化，为理解热塑性材料FSW过程的关键特征提供了有价值的见解，并支持其在工业应用中的更广泛采用。

The effect of HVOF spraying parameters on the tribological properties of the Al 65 Cu 20 Fe 15 coatingsHVOF喷涂参数对Al 65 Cu 20 Fe 15涂层摩擦学性能的影响

Dilnoza Baltabayeva, Sherzod Kurbanbekov, Ali Çoruh, Gulzhaz Uazyrkhanova, Lyaila Bayatanova, Yulduz Amangeldiyeva

This study investigates the effect of oxygen pressure during high-velocity oxygen fuel (HVOF) spraying on the microstructure, phase evolution, and tribological performance of Al 65 Cu 20 Fe 15 quasi-crystalline coatings deposited on American Iron and Steel Institute (AISI) 321 stainless steel. Oxygen pressure was varied between 3.0, 3.5, and 4.0 bar while all other processing parameters were kept constant. Microstructural analysis shows that oxygen pressure significantly influences particle deformation, splat formation, and coating densification. An intermediate pressure of 3.5 bar produces a dense and homogeneous microstructure with reduced porosity and improved inter-splat cohesion. X-ray diffraction confirms the coexistence of quasi-crystalline and intermetallic phases, with the highest quasi-crystalline phase fraction obtained at 3.5 bar. Tribological tests under dry sliding conditions reveal a strong correlation between microstructure and frictional behavior. The coating deposited at 3.5 bar exhibits stable tribological performance with a coefficient of friction of 0.55–0.60 and the lowest wear rate. The 3.0 bar coating shows higher friction and wear due to insufficient particle heating and weak cohesion, while the 4.0 bar coating exhibits lower average friction (0.48–0.52) but unstable behavior and increased wear due to oxidation-induced brittleness. All coatings demonstrate significantly enhanced hardness (>750 HV) compared to the substrate, while adhesion strength is maximized at 3.5 bar. Overall, 3.5 bar is identified as the optimal oxygen pressure for producing Al 65 Cu 20 Fe 15 quasi-crystalline coatings with balanced wear resistance, structural integrity, and tribological stability.本研究探讨了超音速火焰（HVOF）喷涂过程中氧气压力对沉积在美国钢铁学会（AISI）321不锈钢上的Al 65 Cu 20 Fe 15准晶涂层微观组织、物相演变及摩擦学性能的影响。氧气压力分别设为3.0、3.5和4.0 bar，其他工艺参数均保持不变。微观组织分析表明，氧气压力显著影响颗粒变形、扁平粒子形成及涂层致密化。中间压力3.5 bar可形成致密且均匀的微观组织，孔隙率降低，扁平粒子间的内聚力增强。X射线衍射证实了准晶相与金属间化合物相共存，其中3.5 bar下获得的准晶相含量最高。干滑动条件下的摩擦学试验显示，微观组织与摩擦行为之间存在很强的相关性。3.5 bar下沉积的涂层表现出稳定的摩擦学性能，摩擦系数为0.55–0.60，且磨损率最低。3.0 bar涂层因颗粒加热不足和内聚力较弱而表现出较高的摩擦系数和磨损率；4.0 bar涂层的平均摩擦系数较低（0.48–0.52），但由于氧化引起的脆化，其摩擦行为不稳定且磨损加剧。所有涂层的硬度（>750 HV）均显著高于基体，而结合强度在3.5 bar时达到最大。总体而言，3.5 bar被确定为制备兼具耐磨性、结构完整性和摩擦学稳定性的Al 65 Cu 20 Fe 15准晶涂层的最佳氧气压力。

The microstructure, tribological properties and corrosion resistances of WC-10Co-4Cr coatings were systematically investigated and compared. V. Yu. Piirainen, L. Suarez –Torres, T. H. Fernandez-Colombie

This study investigates radial friction welding of dissimilar AA2024 aluminum and Cu-DHP copper using a three-dimensional finite element model together with optical metallography, Vickers microhardness measurements, and thermocouple measurements. A coupled thermomechanical model was implemented in ANSYS Workbench 2023 R1 at 1 400 rpm and 30 MPa. The numerical solution predicts an asymmetric thermal field with a maximum of 750 °C at the Al-Cu interface, an equivalent plastic strain of 0.0177 mm/mm, and a maximum total displacement of 0.130 mm. The strain and displacement fields were interpreted together with the optical observations, which indicate localized thermomechanical modification and preferential material flow near the interface. The thermomechanically affected zone (TMAZ) reached approximately 157 HV. Direct comparison with thermocouple measurements and the measured aluminum-side TMAZ width supported the model for the directly comparable quantities. Microhardness provides complementary evidence of localized hardening but does not constitute direct strain measurement or phase identification. The model is useful for interpreting the thermomechanical response of AA2024/Cu-DHP radial friction welds, while the assumptions regarding friction and radiation, the absence of latent heat, and the absence of microstructural-kinetics modelling remain explicit limitations.本研究结合三维有限元模型、光学金相分析、维氏显微硬度测量和热电偶测量，研究异种AA2024铝合金与Cu-DHP铜的径向摩擦焊。在ANSYS Workbench 2023 R1中建立了热力耦合模型，转速为1 400 rpm，压力为30 MPa。数值解预测得到非对称温度场，Al-Cu界面处最高温度为750 °C，等效塑性应变为0.0177 mm/mm，最大总位移为0.130 mm。结合光学观察结果对应变场和位移场进行了解读，结果表明界面附近存在局部热力作用引起的组织变化和优先材料流动。热力影响区（TMAZ）的硬度达到约157 HV。与热电偶测量结果及铝侧TMAZ宽度实测值的直接比较，为模型在这些可直接比较的量上的预测提供了支持。显微硬度为局部硬化提供了补充证据，但不构成直接应变测量或物相鉴定。该模型有助于解读AA2024/Cu-DHP径向摩擦焊接头的热力响应，但有关摩擦和辐射的假设、未考虑潜热以及未开展微观组织动力学建模，仍是其明确的局限性。
