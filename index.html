EFFECT OF CEO2 AND LA2O3 REINFORCEMENTS ON MECHANICAL, TRIBOLOGICAL AND MICROSTRUCTURAL PROPERTIES OF WC–12CO COATING BY HIGH-VELOCITY OXYGEN FUEL DEPOSITION TECHNIQUECeO2和La2O3增强相对超音速火焰喷涂WC–12Co涂层力学、摩擦学及显微组织性能的影响

Satyanarayan Dubey, Shyam Birla, Tribhuwan Kishore Mishra

Decarburization during HVOF deposition reduces the workability of Tungsten Carbide-12% Cobalt (WC-12Co) coatings. This work examines how CeO2 and La2O3 reinforcements affect HVOF-sprayed WC-12Co coatings' mechanical, tribological, and microstructural characteristics. Compared to the unreinforced coating, microstructure, phase composition, bond strength, abrasive wear, coefficient of friction, and microhardness were assessed. CeO2 or La2O3 decreased porosity and finer grain structure without affecting bond strength. Coatings with 1 wt.% CeO2 and La2O3 increased microhardness by 6% and 4%, respectively, compared to the unreinforced WC-12Co coating, which had an adhesive bond strength of 73.60 ± 5 MPa. Both changed coatings improved structural stability, grain refinement, and densification. Minimum wear rate of 3.5 × 10⁻⁵ mm³/N·m was found in WC-12Co-1La2O3, whereas the lowest coefficient of friction was 0.4 in WC-12Co-1CeO2. An oxide layer from reinforcements enhanced wear resistance. SEM investigation of worn surfaces showed debris, dislodged carbide particles, SiC grains, microcracks, and grooves as the predominant wear processes.超音速火焰喷涂（HVOF）沉积过程中的脱碳会降低碳化钨-12%钴（WC-12Co）涂层的加工性能。本研究考察了CeO2和La2O3增强相对HVOF喷涂WC-12Co涂层力学、摩擦学及显微组织特征的影响。以未增强涂层为参照，对显微组织、物相组成、结合强度、磨粒磨损、摩擦系数和显微硬度进行了评估。CeO2或La2O3的加入降低了孔隙率并细化了晶粒，且未影响结合强度。与未增强的WC-12Co涂层相比，添加1 wt.% CeO2和La2O3的涂层显微硬度分别提高了6%和4%；未增强涂层的粘附结合强度为73.60 ± 5 MPa。两种改性涂层均表现出更好的结构稳定性、更细的晶粒和更高的致密度。WC-12Co-1La2O3的磨损率最低，为3.5 × 10⁻⁵ mm³/N·m，而WC-12Co-1CeO2的摩擦系数最低，为0.4。增强相形成的氧化层提高了耐磨性。磨损表面的SEM分析显示，主要磨损过程表现为磨屑、脱落的碳化物颗粒、SiC颗粒、微裂纹和犁沟。

Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于高密度聚乙烯搅拌摩擦焊焊接质量预测的计算建模与机器学习建模方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. T. Sampath kumar, Rajaram Ganesan, M.M. Reddy

Two protective coatings, 80Ni20Cr and NiCrAlY, were deposited on Al 6061-T6 alloy substrates using the Detonation Gun (D-Gun) thermal spray process. The deposited coatings were studied for their microstructure, phase composition, surface roughness, hardness, corrosion behaviour, wettability, and solid particle erosion resistance. Characterisation was done using SEM-EDS, XRD, AFM, nanoindentation, potentiodynamic polarisation, contact angle measurement, and erosion testing. SEM-EDS results showed that both coatings bonded well to the substrate with uniform thickness. The NiCrAlY coating had a denser microstructure with lower porosity compared to 80Ni20Cr. XRD identified CrNi phases with FCC crystal structure in 80Ni20Cr and Al₂NiY phases with orthorhombic structure in NiCrAlY. Nanocrystalline grain structure was observed in both coatings. AFM measurements showed that NiCrAlY had the lowest surface roughness among all three samples. Nanoindentation results showed that NiCrAlY hardness is approximately 10 times higher than 80Ni20Cr. Specifically, NiCrAlY showed a hardness of 7895.36 MPa (731.2 HV), compared with 780.11 MPa (72.25 HV) for 80Ni20Cr and 708.68 MPa for the uncoated Al 6061 substrate. Potentiodynamic polarisation tests in 3.5 wt% NaCl solution confirmed that NiCrAlY recorded the lowest corrosion current density of 6.625 × 10 -7 A/cm² and a corrosion rate of 0.00567 mmpy. The 80Ni20Cr coating gave a corrosion current density of 1.585 × 10 - 6 A/cm² and a corrosion rate of 0.01427 mmpy. Both figures are considerably lower than the bare substrate at 0.5185 mmpy. Contact angle measurements confirmed superhydrophobic behaviour for NiCrAlY and near-superhydrophobic behaviour for 80Ni20Cr. Solid particle erosion test results showed that 80Ni20Cr recorded the lowest mass loss. Under the laboratory conditions used (3.5 wt% NaCl at room temperature; a single erosion condition of 90° impact angle, 40 m/s and 5 min), NiCrAlY showed the better hardness and corrosion resistance, whereas 80Ni20Cr showed the better erosion resistance. Interpretable ensemble learning for tensile strength prediction and welding process window optimization of friction stir-welded 7N01 aluminum alloy用于搅拌摩擦焊7N01铝合金抗拉强度预测与焊接工艺窗口优化的可解释集成学习

This study aims to achieve high-accuracy prediction of the ultimate tensile strength (UTS) of friction stir-welded A7N01P-T4 aluminum alloy joints, elucidate the effects of welding parameters on UTS, and optimize the welding process window. Rotational speed, welding speed, and shoulder plunge depth were used as input variables, with UTS as the output. Machine learning models were developed using 27 experimental samples and evaluated by leave-one-out cross-validation. Comparison of decision tree, K-nearest neighbors, and Gaussian process regression identifies decision tree as the best baseline model, with an R 2 of 0.905. An ensemble learning strategy was subsequently introduced to construct a gradient boosting decision tree, increasing R 2 to 0.966, and grid search optimization of its key hyperparameters further improves R 2 to 0.978. SHapley Additive exPlanations analysis shows that the importance of the welding parameters follows the order of welding speed, rotational speed, and shoulder plunge depth, and the identified parameter effects agree well with the experimental results and observed microstructural evolution. The optimized model predicts fully feasible ranges of all the welding parameters for a joint efficiency of at least 90%, and the shoulder plunge depth acts as a key parameter for further refining the high-strength welding process window.
