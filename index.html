This comprehensive review systematically examines the fundamental principles governing high-temperature oxidation, from thermodynamic foundations and diffusion-controlled kinetics to the protective characteristics of oxide scales. The parabolic rate law, derived from Wagner's theory of diffusion-controlled growth, provides the framework for predicting oxidation kinetics, with rate constants increasing exponentially with temperature as demonstrated by activation energies ranging from 171 kJ/mol for Ti-based refractory alloys to higher values for alumina-forming systems. A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. EFFECT OF ARGON FLOW RATE DURING AIR PLASMA SPRAYING ON THE STRUCTURE AND PROPERTIES OF NICRFEBSIC COATINGS空气等离子喷涂过程中氩气流量对NiCrFeBSiC涂层结构和性能的影响

Dastan Buitkenov, Nurkhat Bimakhan, Nurmakhanbet Raisov

In this work, self-fluxing metal-ceramic coatings based on the NiCrFeBSiC system were obtained by air plasma spraying, and their structural, mechanical, and tribological properties were investigated.. The effect of argon flow rate (1600 and 1800 L/h) during coating formation was consideredIt was found that an increase in argon flow rate leads to an approximately twofold increase in coating thickness and a twofold decrease in porosity. According to the microhardness measurement results, the microhardness was 1015.8 ± 81.5 HV, while the elastic modulus was 203.9 ± 5.4 GPa. Tribological tests were carried out using the ball-on-disk configurationAt an argon flow rate of 1800 L/h, the friction coefficient showed a lower value (≈0.107), indicating improved wear resistance of the coatings. Scratch test results showed a high level of adhesion between the coatings and the substrate. Although the critical load values (Lc) were similar for both regimes, the coatings obtained at 1800 L/h exhibited a more gradual and stable failure behavior.本研究采用空气等离子喷涂制备了基于NiCrFeBSiC体系的自熔性金属陶瓷涂层，并对其结构、力学和摩擦学性能进行了研究。考察了涂层形成过程中氩气流量（1600和1800 L/h）的影响。结果表明，氩气流量的增加会使涂层厚度约增加一倍，同时孔隙率降低一半。根据显微硬度测量结果，显微硬度为1015.8 ± 81.5 HV，而弹性模量为203.9 ± 5.4 GPa。摩擦学测试采用球-盘配置进行。在氩气流量为1800 L/h时，摩擦系数较低（≈0.107），表明涂层的耐磨性有所提高。划痕测试结果表明涂层与基体之间具有较高的结合强度。尽管两种工况下的临界载荷值（Lc）相近，但在1800 L/h条件下制备的涂层表现出更渐进且更稳定的失效行为。

  • Air plasma sprayed self-fluxing NiCrFeBSiC metal-ceramic coatings at two argon flow rates: 1600 and 1800 L/h.在两种氩气流量条件下（1600和1800 L/h）采用空气等离子喷涂制备自熔性NiCrFeBSiC金属陶瓷涂层。
  • Compared microstructure, porosity, thickness, microhardness, elastic modulus, friction, and adhesion.对显微组织、孔隙率、厚度、显微硬度、弹性模量、摩擦和结合强度进行了比较。
  • Used ball-on-disk tribology and scratch testing to assess wear and coating-substrate bonding.采用球-盘摩擦学测试和划痕测试评估耐磨性及涂层-基体结合。

Evidence signal:证据信号： The higher argon flow approximately doubled coating thickness and halved porosity, while lowering the friction coefficient to about 0.107 and maintaining high adhesion with similar critical loads.较高的氩气流量使涂层厚度约增加一倍、孔隙率减半，同时将摩擦系数降低至约0.107，并在临界载荷相近的情况下保持了较高的结合强度。
