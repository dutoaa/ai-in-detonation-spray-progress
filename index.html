Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于高密度聚乙烯搅拌摩擦焊焊接质量预测的计算建模与机器学习建模方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. These findings provide process-driven design guidelines for developing robust HDPE joints and demonstrate how integrating computational modeling with machine learning enables efficient optimization, offering valuable insights into the key characteristics of the FSW process in thermoplastics and supporting its broader adoption in industrial applications.搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）已被认可为一种有效的热塑性材料连接技术。本研究结合计算方法与机器学习方法，对高密度聚乙烯（HDPE）搅拌摩擦焊中的焊接温度、垂直轴向力、材料流动及缺陷形成等特征进行全面的预测研究，并通过实验结果加以验证。研究建立了三维耦合欧拉–拉格朗日（CEL）数值模型，以模拟不同转速、焊接速度及底部预热条件下的HDPE焊接过程。通过实验数据验证了CEL数值模型，结果表明，该模型在预测孔洞形成、热响应和力学响应方面与实验结果具有很高的一致性。结果显示，底面预热可有效减小温度梯度并稳定垂直力波动。定量而言，60 °C底面预热使峰值下压力从1600 N降至接近1100 N，并使峰值温度从70 °C（未预热）升至约100 °C，从而减缓厚度方向的温度梯度并稳定轴向力分布。同时，利用实验数据训练了两个多层感知机人工神经网络（ANN），用于预测温度和轴向力随时间的响应，其R2介于0.91和0.99之间，表明模型在所测试的FSW参数空间内具有较高的精度和较强的泛化能力。CEL计算模拟准确预测了焊接区域形貌，包括搅拌区、热机械影响区和热影响区；而CEL–ANN联合框架能够快速生成工艺图，标示存在缺陷和无缺陷的焊接工艺窗口。在1500 rpm、30 mm/min及60 °C预热条件下获得了最优工艺条件，而在1000 rpm且未预热时，由于形成隧道缺陷，观察到较差的可焊性。这些发现为制备可靠的HDPE接头提供了工艺驱动的设计指导，并展示了计算建模与机器学习相结合如何实现高效优化，为理解热塑性材料FSW工艺的关键特征提供了有价值的见解，并支持其在工业应用中得到更广泛采用。

Microstructure and surface performance of detonation gun sprayed 80Ni20Cr and NiCrAlY coatings on Al 6061-T6 substrate for structural applications面向结构应用的Al 6061-T6基体上爆炸喷涂80Ni20Cr和NiCrAlY涂层的微观结构与表面性能

T. Sampath kumar, Rajaram Ganesan, M.M. Reddy

Two protective coatings, 80Ni20Cr and NiCrAlY, were deposited on Al 6061-T6 alloy substrates using the Detonation Gun (D-Gun) thermal spray process. The deposited coatings were studied for their microstructure, phase composition, surface roughness, hardness, corrosion behaviour, wettability, and solid particle erosion resistance. Characterisation was done using SEM-EDS, XRD, AFM, nanoindentation, potentiodynamic polarisation, contact angle measurement, and erosion testing. SEM-EDS results showed that both coatings bonded well to the substrate with uniform thickness. The NiCrAlY coating had a denser microstructure with lower porosity compared to 80Ni20Cr. XRD identified CrNi phases with FCC crystal structure in 80Ni20Cr and Al₂NiY phases with orthorhombic structure in NiCrAlY. Nanocrystalline grain structure was observed in both coatings. AFM measurements showed that NiCrAlY had the lowest surface roughness among all three samples. Nanoindentation results showed that NiCrAlY hardness is approximately 10 times higher than 80Ni20Cr. Specifically, NiCrAlY showed a hardness of 7895.36 MPa (731.2 HV), compared with 780.11 MPa (72.25 HV) for 80Ni20Cr and 708.68 MPa for the uncoated Al 6061 substrate. Potentiodynamic polarisation tests in 3.5 wt% NaCl solution confirmed that NiCrAlY recorded the lowest corrosion current density of 6.625 × 10 -7 A/cm² and a corrosion rate of 0.00567 mmpy. The 80Ni20Cr coating gave a corrosion current density of 1.585 × 10 - 6 A/cm² and a corrosion rate of 0.01427 mmpy. Both figures are considerably lower than the bare substrate at 0.5185 mmpy. Contact angle measurements confirmed superhydrophobic behaviour for NiCrAlY and near-superhydrophobic behaviour for 80Ni20Cr. Solid particle erosion test results showed that 80Ni20Cr recorded the lowest mass loss. Under the laboratory conditions used (3.5 wt% NaCl at room temperature; a single erosion condition of 90° impact angle, 40 m/s and 5 min), NiCrAlY showed the better hardness and corrosion resistance, whereas 80Ni20Cr showed the better erosion resistance. Physics-informed multi-sensor fusion framework for predicting weld penetration state and mechanistic discovery in CMT welding用于CMT焊接熔透状态预测与机理发现的物理信息引导多传感器融合框架

Yu Zhang, Kai Yang, Xudong Niu, Kailong Meng, Wenlong Zheng, Jiadui Chen

Accurate recognition of weld states is essential for ensuring structural integrity in cold metal transfer (CMT) welding. However, it remains challenging to develop models that are both robust to industrial disturbances and physically interpretable. In addition, the inherent opacity of current data-driven methods often limits their reliability in complex manufacturing environments. This study proposes ASPIS-Net, a robust physics-informed network for interpretable CMT weld-state monitoring. The proposed network adopts a dual-branch architecture with an adaptive gated fusion mechanism, which dynamically suppresses environmental noise while preserving critical acoustic and electrical features. To improve model transparency, a symbolic regression framework is further introduced to convert the learned representations into explicit physical equations, thereby aligning deep-learning predictions with the underlying welding thermodynamics. The proposed method was validated using an automated CMT welding platform, from which 5,000 synchronised acoustic and electrical signal samples were collected under four typical penetration states. The results show that ASPIS-Net achieves a classification accuracy of 99.36% and retains a Macro-F1 score of 75.59% under the 0 dB composite-noise condition. This work provides a transparent, physics-guided approach for monitoring multi-physics manufacturing processes, establishing a rational basis for assessing structural reliability in critical welded joints.准确识别焊接状态对于确保冷金属过渡（CMT）焊接的结构完整性至关重要。然而，开发既能稳健应对工业干扰、又具有物理可解释性的模型仍然面临挑战。此外，现有数据驱动方法固有的不透明性往往限制了其在复杂制造环境中的可靠性。本研究提出了ASPIS-Net，这是一种稳健的物理信息引导网络，用于可解释的CMT焊接状态监测。该网络采用具有自适应门控融合机制的双分支架构，在保留关键声学与电学特征的同时动态抑制环境噪声。为提高模型透明度，进一步引入符号回归框架，将学习到的表征转化为显式物理方程，从而使深度学习预测与焊接过程的内在热力学机制相一致。所提方法通过自动化CMT焊接平台进行了验证，在四种典型熔透状态下共采集了5,000个同步声学与电学信号样本。结果表明，ASPIS-Net的分类准确率达到99.36%，且在0 dB复合噪声条件下仍保持75.59%的Macro-F1得分。本研究为多物理场制造过程监测提供了一种透明、物理引导的方法，为评估关键焊接接头的结构可靠性奠定了合理基础。

Effect of Laser Remelting on the Microstructure, Mechanical and Tribological Properties of Detonation-Sprayed NiCrFeBSiC Coatings for Mechanical Engineering Applications激光重熔对机械工程用爆炸喷涂NiCrFeBSiC涂层微观组织、力学性能和摩擦学性能的影响

This study investigates the effect of laser remelting on the microstructure, phase composition, mechanical properties, tribological behavior, and corrosion resistance of self-fluxing NiCrFeBSiC coatings deposited onto AISI 321 stainless steel by detonation spraying. The coatings were characterized before and after laser treatment using scanning electron microscopy (SEM), energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS), X-ray diffraction (XRD), instrumented indentation, adhesion testing, ball-on-disk tribometry, and potentiodynamic polarization measurements. SEM analysis revealed that laser remelting reduced coating porosity from 0.306 ± 0.014% to 0.15 ± 0.09%, accompanied by microstructural densification and a reduction in coating thickness. XRD analysis confirmed the preservation of the principal crystalline phases, including Ni3Fe, Cr7C3, CrB, and Ni3B, following laser treatment. The Vickers hardness increased from 1049.1 ± 43.4 to 1206.1 ± 63.1 HV, while the elastic modulus increased from 233.6 ± 6.2 to 258.4 ± 5.3 GPa. Furthermore, adhesion strength increased by 19.4%, whereas surface roughness decreased by 19.2%. Electrochemical measurements indicated a modest decrease in corrosion current density of approximately 3.0%, accompanied by changes in the morphology of corrosion-affected regions.
