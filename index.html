Feasibility study of using magnetic and thermoelectric methods for the assessment of storage-induced changes in flux-cored welding wires利用磁性和热电方法评估药芯焊丝储存诱导变化的可行性研究

Aleksandra Świerczyńska, Balázs Varbai, Leszek Piotrowski

The lack of knowledge regarding whether a welding consumable remains suitable for the welding process or has undergone degradation that precludes its further use constitutes a significant challenge. Appropriate control procedures and effective evaluation methods may contribute to addressing this issue. This study presents the results of investigations concerning the applicability of magnetic and thermoelectric methods for such an assessment. Rutile flux-cored welding wires were stored in four different locations and subsequently collected for testing after 3, 6, and 12 months. Their suitability for industrial application was initially assessed on the basis of visual examination. Subsequently, measurements of thermoelectric power, characterization of bulk magnetic properties, and Magnetic Barkhausen Noise (MBN) were performed for each wire. Visual inspection confirmed the good surface condition of the wires despite the exceedance of the recommended limits of temperature and relative humidity during storage. The thermoelectric measurements showed that storage environment significantly affects the Seebeck Coefficient (SC) of welding wires, with marine conditions inducing rapid changes and less aggressive environments producing a gradual time dependent increase. Magnetic property measurements indicated that two of the evaluated parameters, namely the amplitude of MBN and the area under the envelopes of MBN, exhibited changes with storage time at each exposure site. Statistical analysis demonstrated that the parameters recorded during magnetic and thermoelectric measurements were characterized by a high degree of correlation, exceeding 0.9 in some cases. A strong linear relationship between the MBN Amplitude-to-Envelope Ratio and SC was observed for wires stored in the Urban Area and Workshop environments (r = 0.99 for both), indicating a close correlation between the evolution of their thermoelectric and magnetic properties during storage. Optimization of Friction Stir Welding Parameters for Improving the Mechanical Properties of Aluminum Alloy Joints优化搅拌摩擦焊参数以提升铝合金接头力学性能

Pompy Pratisna, Ferdy Hendarto Susilo, Sutikno Wahyu Hidayat, Dedy Aryanto

Friction stir welding (FSW) performance depends on the interaction among processing parameters rather than individual operating conditions. This study examined the effects of rotational speed (800, 1000, and 1200 rpm), travel speed (40, 60, and 80 mm/min), and axial force (4, 6, and 8 kN) on the mechanical properties of 6-mm AA6061-T6 butt joints. A 3 × 3 × 3 full factorial design with three reported replicates per condition yielded 81 mechanical-test observations, covering ultimate tensile strength (UTS), yield strength, elongation, and hardness. Factorial analysis of variance (ANOVA) and coded quadratic regression were performed using IBM SPSS Statistics 31. All three parameters showed significant main effects across the four mechanical responses (p < .001), while the Rotation × Travel interaction was significant for each response. Rotational speed exhibited the largest partial effect size for UTS (partial η² = .807). The quadratic regression models explained 72.1%–84.6% of the response variance and identified 1200 rpm, 80 mm/min, and 8 kN as the highest-predicted UTS setting among the 27 evaluated combinations, with a predicted UTS of 280.39 MPa and a calculated joint efficiency of 90.45%. These findings demonstrate the importance of considering parameter interactions when selecting FSW operating conditions, while limiting the model-based recommendation to the investigated material, tool configuration, and experimental range.搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）性能取决于加工参数之间的相互作用，而非单个操作条件。本研究考察了转速（800、1000 和 1200 rpm）、行进速度（40、60 和 80 mm/min）以及轴向力（4、6 和 8 kN）对 6 mm AA6061-T6 对接接头力学性能的影响。采用 3 × 3 × 3 全因子设计，并在每种条件下报告 3 次重复，共获得 81 个力学测试观测值，涵盖抗拉强度（UTS）、屈服强度、伸长率和硬度。使用 IBM SPSS Statistics 31 进行了因子方差分析（ANOVA）和编码二次回归。三项参数在四个力学响应上均表现出显著主效应（p < .001），同时 Rotation × Travel 交互作用对每个响应均显著。对于 UTS，转速表现出最大的部分效应量（partial η² = .807）。二次回归模型解释了 72.1%–84.6% 的响应方差，并在所评估的 27 种组合中确定 1200 rpm、80 mm/min 和 8 kN 为预测 UTS 最高的设置，预测 UTS 为 280.39 MPa，计算得到的接头效率为 90.45%。这些结果表明，在选择 FSW 操作条件时考虑参数交互作用十分重要，但基于模型的推荐仅适用于所研究的材料、工具配置和实验范围。

Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于高密度聚乙烯摩擦搅拌焊焊缝质量预测的计算与机器学习建模方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation.
