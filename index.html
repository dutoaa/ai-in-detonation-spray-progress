Deep Learning-Based Welding Process Optimization and Adaptive Control Method for Welding Robots基于深度学习的焊接机器人焊接工艺优化与自适应控制方法

Hai-Tao Li, Tian Xiao-long, Peng Zhang, Feng Yang, Tao Wang, Ci-En Li

GMAW (gas metal arc welding) robots are frequently used in contemporary manufacturing as they offer high levels of productivity, repeatable process stability and less need for manual labour. However, even today practical robotic welding is often based upon manual design, teaching and offline programming. Welding parameters and trajectories must be redesigned whenever the workpiece, joint geometries or assembly conditions change. Furthermore, during welding, changes to the weld-seam state caused by machining error, assembly deviation and cyclic heat input result in weld parameters planned for ideal conditions becoming inadequate and the risk of incomplete fusion, porosity, cracking, slag inclusions and unstable backside formations greatly increased. In this paper a method based on deep learning is presented to provide optimization of the welding process and adaptive control for GMAW robots. This method consists of integrated quality grade oriented process planning; site-specifically improved multi-objective parameter optimization; semi-supervised semantic segmentation for the perception of weld seams and molten pools and their associated spatter suppression through neural networks predicting weld formation; and fuzzy adaptive control logic. Butt joints of Q235 carbon structural steel were tested experimentally and a response surface model was developed linking welding current, welding speed, torch angle and nozzle height to geometric properties of the welds.GMAW（熔化极气体保护电弧焊）机器人因其生产效率高、工艺稳定性可重复且对人工劳动的需求较少，在现代制造业中得到广泛应用。然而，即使在今天，实际机器人焊接仍往往依赖人工设计、示教和离线编程。每当工件、接头几何形状或装配条件发生变化时，都必须重新设计焊接参数和轨迹。此外，在焊接过程中，加工误差、装配偏差和周期性热输入引起的焊缝状态变化，会使针对理想条件规划的焊接参数不再适用，并大幅增加未熔合、气孔、裂纹、夹渣及背面成形不稳定的风险。本文提出了一种基于深度学习的方法，用于GMAW机器人的焊接工艺优化与自适应控制。该方法集成了面向质量等级的工艺规划、针对现场情况改进的多目标参数优化、用于焊缝和熔池感知的半监督语义分割及通过预测焊缝成形的神经网络实现的相关飞溅抑制，以及模糊自适应控制逻辑。研究对Q235碳素结构钢对接接头进行了试验，并建立了响应面模型，将焊接电流、焊接速度、焊枪角度和喷嘴高度与焊缝几何特征关联起来。

Recent Advances in Friction Stir Welding of Aluminum Alloys and Aluminum Metal Matrix Composites: Process Developments, Microstructural Evolution, and Mechanical Performance铝合金及铝基金属基复合材料搅拌摩擦焊的最新进展：工艺发展、微观组织演变与力学性能

Shailesh S. Parkhe, Javed Sikandar Shaikh, Amit Malgol, Mayur Jayant Gitay, Aniket Akhade, Aqleem Siddiqui

Friction stir welding (FSW) is an established solid-state joining process for aluminium alloys and aluminium metal matrix composites (Al-MMCs), offering advantages over conventional fusion welding by reducing melting-related defects, distortion, and thermal degradation. However, weld quality and mechanical performance are governed by the coupled effects of process parameters, tool characteristics, thermal conditions, material flow, and, in Al-MMCs, reinforcement behaviour. Although several recent reviews have examined individual aspects of FSW, a comprehensive comparative synthesis linking process parameters and thermal/deformation mechanisms with microstructural evolution, defect formation, tool wear, and mechanical performance across conventional and advanced FSW of both aluminium alloys and Al-MMCs remains limited. This review addresses this gap by critically examining conventional FSW and selected advanced variants, including underwater/submerged FSW, vibration- and ultrasonic-assisted FSW, laser-assisted FSW, and electrical current-assisted FSW, from an integrated process–structure–property perspective. The central contribution of this review is a mechanism-oriented comparative framework that identifies how the different FSW approaches modify heat generation, heat dissipation, material plasticization, and material flow, and how these changes subsequently influence microstructure, defects, tool wear, residual stress, and mechanical performance. Particular emphasis is placed on rotational speed, traverse speed, cooling conditions, tool geometry and material, external energy assistance, dynamic recrystallization, reinforcement redistribution, and reinforcement–tool interactions. The comparison demonstrates that the effectiveness of each FSW variant is strongly dependent on the material system and processing conditions, limiting the applicability of universal process guidelines. For Al-MMCs, reinforcement fraction, particle size and distribution, particle fragmentation or clustering, and tool–reinforcement interactions introduce additional challenges compared with conventional aluminium alloys. Despite significant advances, challenges remain in controlling thermal conditions, achieving uniform material flow, detecting internal defects, minimizing tool wear, and translating advanced FSW variants from laboratory-scale investigations to industrial production. Welding quality is a critical factor in industrial equipment manufacturing, directly influencing structural integrity, operational reliability, and component safety. However, welding discontinuities may result from the interaction of technical, operational, organizational, and environmental factors, requiring integrated preventive quality management approaches. This study aims to analyze the main factors influencing weld quality in industrial equipment manufacturing, systematically classifying potential causes of discontinuities and identifying preventive measures based on technical standards. The research adopts a qualitative, exploratory, and descriptive approach, supported by a literature review and documentary analysis of welding quality standards, particularly the ABNT NBR ISO 3834 series. The Ishikawa Diagram, structured according to the 6M framework, was employed to classify potential causes into Method, Machine, Material, Manpower, Measurement, and Environment. The findings identified five major welding discontinuities: porosity, cracks, lack of fusion, incomplete penetration, and undercut. The analysis indicates that these discontinuities may arise from interconnected process conditions, emphasizing the importance of standardized procedures, equipment monitoring, appropriate material handling, personnel qualification, and systematic inspection. The study contributes an integrated analytical framework linking welding discontinuities, potential causal factors, and preventive control measures. Study on the transition behavior and forming process of oscillating laser double-wire welding of aluminum alloy铝合金振荡激光双丝焊接过渡行为与成形过程研究

Xiaoxu Li, Quanda Zhang, Dandan Zhao, Zizhi Liu, Wenhao Huang

To achieve high-quality and high-stability weld formation in aluminum alloys, this study investigates the dynamic evolution of the molten pool and droplet transfer behavior during oscillating laser double-wire welding. Particular emphasis is placed on the coupled effects of process parameters—including laser power, laser oscillation parameters (frequency, amplitude, and trajectory), and wire feeding speed—on molten-pool morphology and weld quality. By employing high-speed imaging technology, the surface flow of the molten pool, droplet transfer, and metal filling behavior were captured in real time, revealing the influence of oscillation parameters on the stability of weld formation. Research indicates that different laser oscillation patterns—such as circular, ∞-shaped, and figure-eight trajectories—significantly modify the spatial distribution and fluctuation period of heat-source energy within the molten pool, thereby influencing the pool’s responsiveness to external energy input and material feed. A well-designed oscillation trajectory can effectively enhance convective flow within the molten pool and improve the stability of droplet transfer. This research further aims to construct a mapping relationship between droplet transfer and molten-pool response under various process parameters, and to establish a multifactor coupled model of “energy input–molten pool behavior-forming quality” providing both theoretical support and experimental evidence for high-precision control and process optimization in oscillating laser double-wire welding.为实现铝合金焊缝的高质量、高稳定性成形，本研究考察了振荡激光双丝焊接过程中熔池的动态演化与熔滴过渡行为，重点关注激光功率、激光振荡参数（频率、振幅和轨迹）以及送丝速度等工艺参数对熔池形貌与焊缝质量的耦合影响。采用高速成像技术，实时捕捉熔池表面流动、熔滴过渡及金属填充行为，揭示振荡参数对焊缝成形稳定性的影响。研究表明，圆形、∞形和8字形等不同激光振荡轨迹会显著改变熔池内热源能量的空间分布与波动周期，进而影响熔池对外部能量输入和材料供给的响应特性。合理设计的振荡轨迹可有效增强熔池内的对流流动，提高熔滴过渡的稳定性。本研究还旨在构建不同工艺参数下熔滴过渡与熔池响应之间的映射关系，并建立“能量输入—熔池行为—成形质量”多因素耦合模型，为振荡激光双丝焊接的高精度控制与工艺优化提供理论支撑和实验证据。
