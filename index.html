Post-processing strategies for generative design outcomes in a UAV tail boom无人机尾梁生成式设计结果的后处理策略

Michał Piotr Kowalik, Piotr Stapinski, Mateusz Papis

Generative design produces structurally efficient geometries whose complex, organic shapes require post-processing before they can be manufactured by fused filament fabrication (FFF). This paper compares two post-processing strategies applied to the same generative design outcome for the tail boom of an unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV). The first, complex variant follows the raw topology closely; the second, simplified variant uses symmetric arms, self-supporting geometry, and uniform cross-sections oriented toward FFF manufacturability. Both variants were evaluated by finite element analysis (FEA) comprising linear static and buckling studies under identical loading conditions, and scaled prototypes of the simplified variant were printed in two bounding infill configurations and load-tested. The simplification reduced mass by 4% and print time by 20% while eliminating support structures, at the cost of a 31% increase in peak stress, which remained below 2% of the ultimate tensile strength assumed in the FEA model. Stress concentrations predicted by FEA were qualitatively consistent with the fracture locations observed in testing. Within the studied case and under the analyzed quasi-static loads, the stress increase did not change the assessment of load-bearing adequacy, indicating that manufacturing-oriented simplification of generative design outcomes can improve printability while reducing print time and material use.生成式设计能够产生结构效率高的几何形状，但其复杂的有机形态需要经过后处理，才能采用熔融丝材制造（FFF）工艺制造。本文针对无人机（UAV）尾梁的同一生成式设计结果，比较了两种后处理策略。第一种为复杂方案，紧密遵循原始拓扑；第二种为简化方案，采用对称臂、自支撑几何形状和均匀截面，以适应FFF制造要求。在相同载荷条件下，通过包含线性静力分析和屈曲分析的有限元分析（FEA）对两种方案进行了评估，并采用两种界限填充配置打印简化方案的缩比原型，开展承载试验。简化使质量降低了4%，打印时间缩短了20%，同时无需支撑结构，代价是峰值应力增加了31%，但该应力仍低于FEA模型中假定的极限抗拉强度的2%。FEA预测的应力集中位置与试验中观察到的断裂位置在定性上相符。在所研究的案例及所分析的准静态载荷条件下，应力增加未改变对承载能力充足性的评估，表明面向制造的生成式设计结果简化能够改善可打印性，同时减少打印时间和材料用量。

Spatial characteristic of temperature distribution and material flow behaviors during bobbin tool friction stir welding: a computational fluid dynamics study双轴肩搅拌摩擦焊过程中温度分布与材料流动行为的空间特征：一项计算流体力学研究

Honghua Han, Xu Yan, Yanhua Zhao, Suhong Zhang, Qingyu Shi, Chengle Yang

The incorporation of dual shoulders endows bobbin tool friction stir welding (BT-FSW) with spatially distinct heat transfer and material flow characteristics relative to conventional FSW. The vague understanding of these processes hinders efficient optimization of BT-FSW. To address this gap, a computational fluid dynamics (CFD) model, which considered the fully coupling of thermo-mechanical-fluid, was established. Three contact pressure models, namely the Front-Peaked Contact Pressure Model (FP-CPM), Uniform Contact Pressure Model (U-CPM), and Rear-Peaked Contact Pressure Model (RP-CPM), were proposed to model the pressure distribution at the tool-workpiece (T/W) interface. The results show that the distribution of contact pressure at the T/W interface constitutes a critical factor governing the temperature field and flow behavior during welding stage. The FP-CPM provided superior predictive accuracy for the welding temperature, the shape of plastic deformation zone, and material flow trajectories, compared to U-CPM and RP-CPM. Agreed by both experiment and prediction, the temperature on the retreating side (RS) exceeded that on the advancing side (AS). Revealed by FP-CPM model, this phenomenon is attributable to the deflection of high-temperature material from the AS toward the RS, driven by the intense material flow ahead of the tool induced by the high-pressure zone. At the T/W interface, the material exhibited asymmetric horizontal flow with a thin layer ahead of the tool and a broader low-speed region behind the tool. Concurrently, vertical migration featured circumferential flow around the tool converging toward the plate mid-thickness.与常规搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）相比，双轴肩结构使双轴肩搅拌摩擦焊（BT-FSW）具有不同的空间传热与材料流动特征。对这些过程认识不清，阻碍了BT-FSW的高效优化。为弥补这一不足，建立了考虑热–力–流体完全耦合的计算流体力学（CFD）模型。提出了三种接触压力模型，即前部峰值接触压力模型（Front-Peaked Contact Pressure Model，FP-CPM）、均匀接触压力模型（Uniform Contact Pressure Model，U-CPM）和后部峰值接触压力模型（Rear-Peaked Contact Pressure Model，RP-CPM），用于模拟搅拌头–工件（T/W）界面的压力分布。结果表明，T/W界面的接触压力分布是决定焊接阶段温度场与流动行为的关键因素。与U-CPM和RP-CPM相比，FP-CPM对焊接温度、塑性变形区形状及材料流动轨迹的预测更为准确。实验与预测均表明，后退侧（RS）的温度高于前进侧（AS）。FP-CPM模型揭示，这一现象源于高压区诱发的搅拌头前方强烈材料流动，该流动驱使高温材料从AS向RS偏转。在T/W界面处，材料呈现不对称的水平流动，搅拌头前方形成薄层，后方则形成较宽的低速区域。同时，竖向迁移表现为绕搅拌头的周向流动向板厚中部汇聚。

Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于预测高密度聚乙烯搅拌摩擦焊焊接质量的计算建模与机器学习建模方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. These findings provide process-driven design guidelines for developing robust HDPE joints and demonstrate how integrating computational modeling with machine learning enables efficient optimization, offering valuable insights into the key characteristics of the FSW process in thermoplastics and supporting its broader adoption in industrial applications.搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）已被认可为一种连接热塑性材料的有效技术。本研究综合采用计算方法和机器学习方法，对高密度聚乙烯（HDPE）搅拌摩擦焊中的焊接温度、垂直轴向力、材料流动和缺陷形成等特征进行预测，并通过实验结果加以验证。研究建立了三维耦合欧拉–拉格朗日（CEL）数值模型，用于模拟不同转速、焊接速度和底部预热条件下的HDPE焊接过程。CEL数值模型通过实验数据得到验证，在孔洞形成以及热响应和力学响应的预测方面表现出很好的吻合度。结果表明，底面预热能够有效减小温度梯度，并抑制垂直力波动。定量而言，60 °C底面预热使峰值下压力从1600 N降至接近1100 N，并使峰值温度从未预热时的70 °C升至约100 °C，从而减缓厚度方向的温度梯度，使轴向力分布更加稳定。同时，利用实验数据训练了两个多层感知机人工神经网络（ANN），以预测温度和轴向力随时间变化的响应，其R2介于0.91和0.99之间，表明它们在所测试的FSW参数空间内具有较高的精度和较强的泛化能力。CEL计算模拟准确预测了焊接区域的形貌，包括搅拌区、热机械影响区和热影响区；而CEL–ANN联合框架能够快速生成工艺图，标示有缺陷和无缺陷的焊接工艺窗口。在所测试条件下，最优条件为1500 rpm、30 mm/min和60 °C预热；在1000 rpm且未预热时，由于形成虫孔缺陷，观察到较差的可焊性。这些发现为制备可靠的HDPE接头提供了以工艺为导向的设计指南，并展示了计算建模与机器学习相结合如何实现高效优化，为理解热塑性材料FSW工艺的关键特征提供了有价值的见解，同时支持其在工业应用中的更广泛采用。

Microstructure and surface performance of detonation gun sprayed 80Ni20Cr and NiCrAlY coatings on Al 6061-T6 substrate for structural applications面向结构应用的 Al 6061-T6 基体上爆炸喷涂 80Ni20Cr 和 NiCrAlY 涂层的微观组织与表面性能

T. Sampath kumar, Rajaram Ganesan, M.M. Reddy

Two protective coatings, 80Ni20Cr and NiCrAlY, were deposited on Al 6061-T6 alloy substrates using the Detonation Gun (D-Gun) thermal spray process. The deposited coatings were studied for their microstructure, phase composition, surface roughness, hardness, corrosion behaviour, wettability, and solid particle erosion resistance. Characterisation was done using SEM-EDS, XRD, AFM, nanoindentation, potentiodynamic polarisation, contact angle measurement, and erosion testing. SEM-EDS results showed that both coatings bonded well to the substrate with uniform thickness. The NiCrAlY coating had a denser microstructure with lower porosity compared to 80Ni20Cr. XRD identified CrNi phases with FCC crystal structure in 80Ni20Cr and Al₂NiY phases with orthorhombic structure in NiCrAlY. Nanocrystalline grain structure was observed in both coatings. AFM measurements showed that NiCrAlY had the lowest surface roughness among all three samples. Nanoindentation results showed that NiCrAlY hardness is approximately 10 times higher than 80Ni20Cr. Specifically, NiCrAlY showed a hardness of 7895.36 MPa (731.2 HV), compared with 780.11 MPa (72.25 HV) for 80Ni20Cr and 708.68 MPa for the uncoated Al 6061 substrate. Potentiodynamic polarisation tests in 3.5 wt% NaCl solution confirmed that NiCrAlY recorded the lowest corrosion current density of 6.625 × 10 -7 A/cm² and a corrosion rate of 0.00567 mmpy. The 80Ni20Cr coating gave a corrosion current density of 1.585 × 10 - 6 A/cm² and a corrosion rate of 0.01427 mmpy. Both figures are considerably lower than the bare substrate at 0.5185 mmpy. Contact angle measurements confirmed superhydrophobic behaviour for NiCrAlY and near-superhydrophobic behaviour for 80Ni20Cr. Solid particle erosion test results showed that 80Ni20Cr recorded the lowest mass loss. Under the laboratory conditions used (3.5 wt% NaCl at room temperature; a single erosion condition of 90° impact angle, 40 m/s and 5 min), NiCrAlY showed the better hardness and corrosion resistance, whereas 80Ni20Cr showed the better erosion resistance.
