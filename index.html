Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于高密度聚乙烯搅拌摩擦焊焊接质量预测的计算建模与机器学习建模方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. These findings provide process-driven design guidelines for developing robust HDPE joints and demonstrate how integrating computational modeling with machine learning enables efficient optimization, offering valuable insights into the key characteristics of the FSW process in thermoplastics and supporting its broader adoption in industrial applications.搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）已被认可为连接热塑性材料的有效技术。本研究结合计算与机器学习方法，对高密度聚乙烯（HDPE）搅拌摩擦焊中的焊接温度、竖直轴向力、材料流动及缺陷形成等特征开展了全面的预测研究，并通过实验结果进行验证。研究建立了三维耦合欧拉–拉格朗日（CEL）数值模型，用于模拟不同转速、焊接速度及底部预热条件下的HDPE焊接过程。通过实验数据验证了CEL数值模型，结果表明，该模型在预测孔洞形成以及热响应和力学响应方面与实验结果具有很高的一致性。结果显示，底面预热能够有效减小温度梯度，并稳定竖直方向的力波动。定量而言，60 °C底面预热使峰值下压力从1600 N降至接近1100 N，并使峰值温度从未预热时的70 °C升至约100 °C，从而减缓厚度方向的温度梯度，并稳定轴向力分布。同时，利用实验数据训练了两个多层感知机人工神经网络（ANN），用于预测温度和轴向力随时间的响应，R2介于0.91和0.99之间，表明其在所测试的FSW参数空间内具有较高的准确性和较强的泛化能力。CEL计算模拟准确预测了焊接区域的形貌，包括搅拌区、热机械影响区和热影响区；而CEL–ANN联合框架则能够快速生成工艺图谱，标示有缺陷和无缺陷的焊接工艺窗口。在1500 rpm、30 mm/min及60 °C预热条件下获得了最优工艺条件，而在1000 rpm且不预热时，由于形成虫孔缺陷，观察到较差的焊接性。这些发现为制备可靠的HDPE接头提供了以工艺为导向的设计指南，并展示了计算建模与机器学习相结合如何实现高效优化，为理解热塑性材料FSW工艺的关键特征提供了有价值的见解，并支持其在工业应用中的更广泛采用。

This study investigates the effect of oxygen pressure during high-velocity oxygen fuel (HVOF) spraying on the microstructure, phase evolution, and tribological performance of Al 65 Cu 20 Fe 15 quasi-crystalline coatings deposited on American Iron and Steel Institute (AISI) 321 stainless steel. Oxygen pressure was varied between 3.0, 3.5, and 4.0 bar while all other processing parameters were kept constant. Microstructural analysis shows that oxygen pressure significantly influences particle deformation, splat formation, and coating densification. An intermediate pressure of 3.5 bar produces a dense and homogeneous microstructure with reduced porosity and improved inter-splat cohesion. X-ray diffraction confirms the coexistence of quasi-crystalline and intermetallic phases, with the highest quasi-crystalline phase fraction obtained at 3.5 bar. Tribological tests under dry sliding conditions reveal a strong correlation between microstructure and frictional behavior. The coating deposited at 3.5 bar exhibits stable tribological performance with a coefficient of friction of 0.55–0.60 and the lowest wear rate. The 3.0 bar coating shows higher friction and wear due to insufficient particle heating and weak cohesion, while the 4.0 bar coating exhibits lower average friction (0.48–0.52) but unstable behavior and increased wear due to oxidation-induced brittleness. All coatings demonstrate significantly enhanced hardness (>750 HV) compared to the substrate, while adhesion strength is maximized at 3.5 bar. Overall, 3.5 bar is identified as the optimal oxygen pressure for producing Al 65 Cu 20 Fe 15 quasi-crystalline coatings with balanced wear resistance, structural integrity, and tribological stability.本研究考察了超音速火焰（HVOF）喷涂过程中氧气压力对沉积在美国钢铁学会（AISI）321不锈钢上的Al 65 Cu 20 Fe 15准晶涂层的微观组织、相演变及摩擦学性能的影响。氧气压力分别设为3.0、3.5和4.0 bar，其他所有工艺参数均保持恒定。微观组织分析表明，氧气压力显著影响颗粒变形、扁平粒子形成及涂层致密化。中间压力3.5 bar下形成了致密、均匀的微观组织，孔隙率降低，扁平粒子间的内聚结合得到改善。X射线衍射证实了准晶相与金属间化合物相共存，其中3.5 bar下获得的准晶相含量最高。干滑动条件下的摩擦学试验表明，微观组织与摩擦行为之间存在很强的相关性。3.5 bar下沉积的涂层表现出稳定的摩擦学性能，摩擦系数为0.55–0.60，磨损率最低。3.0 bar下制备的涂层由于颗粒受热不足、内聚结合较弱，摩擦系数和磨损率较高；而4.0 bar下制备的涂层虽然平均摩擦系数较低（0.48–0.52），但因氧化引起的脆化而表现不稳定，且磨损加剧。与基体相比，所有涂层的硬度均显著提高（>750 HV），而结合强度在3.5 bar下达到最大值。总体而言，3.5 bar被确定为制备兼顾耐磨性、结构完整性和摩擦学稳定性的Al 65 Cu 20 Fe 15准晶涂层的最佳氧气压力。
