This study investigates the effects of loading angle and build direction on the tensile properties of components fabricated through wire arc additive manufacturing (WAAM) fabricated using ER70S-6 steel. Tensile tests were conducted at four loading angles (0°, 45°, 60°, and 90°) for two build directions (flat and upright). For each loading angle and build direction, two tensile specimens were tested, and the reported values were obtained from a representative specimen for each testing condition. The results reveal that the WAAM-fabricated specimens exhibited pronounced mechanical anisotropy, with the highest tensile strength obtained when the applied load is parallel to the bead direction, followed by a considerable reduction at other loading angles. For the upright build direction, the ultimate tensile strength decreased by approximately 47% at 450 relative to that at 0°, suggesting a possible contribution from interlayer shear stresses and delamination-related failure mechanisms. An empirical model was proposed to describe the dependence of tensile strength on the loading angle, enabling the quantitative evaluation of the role of different stress components. The flat build direction exhibited more stable mechanical behavior and a lower degree of anisotropy. In addition, hardness measurements showed that the variation in hardness was mainly governed by the build direction rather than the loading angle. The obtained results provide further insight into the relationship among layered structure, stress state, and mechanical properties, thereby supporting the design and application of WAAM structures subjected to multidirectional loading conditions.本研究考察了加载角度和构建方向对采用ER70S-6钢通过电弧增材制造（WAAM）制备的构件拉伸性能的影响。针对两种构建方向（平放和直立），在四种加载角度（0°、45°、60°和90°）下进行了拉伸试验。每种加载角度和构建方向组合均测试了两个拉伸试样，所报告的数值取自各试验条件下的一个代表性试样。结果表明，WAAM制备的试样表现出显著的力学各向异性：当施加载荷平行于焊道方向时，抗拉强度最高，而在其他加载角度下则明显降低。对于直立构建方向，加载角度为450时的极限抗拉强度较0°时降低约47%，表明层间剪应力和与分层相关的失效机制可能对此有所贡献。研究提出了一个经验模型，用于描述抗拉强度对加载角度的依赖关系，从而能够定量评估不同应力分量的作用。平放构建方向表现出更稳定的力学行为和更低程度的各向异性。此外，硬度测量表明，硬度变化主要受构建方向而非加载角度支配。所得结果进一步揭示了层状结构、应力状态与力学性能之间的关系，从而为承受多向载荷条件的WAAM结构的设计与应用提供支持。

Compensation of Linear Misalignments in EN AW-6082-T6 Aluminium Sheets by Multi-Pin Wire-Based Friction Stir Welding采用多搅拌针送丝式搅拌摩擦焊补偿EN AW-6082-T6铝板的线性错边

Stefan Donaubauer, Amin Ghobishavi, Stefan Weihe, Martin Werz

Linear misalignment (LM) can arise from dimensional tolerances, positioning inaccuracies, limited component stiffness, and clamping constraints, particularly in large-scale welded structures. While filler-assisted fusion welding provides some flexibility for accommodating such geometrical deviations, conventional friction stir welding (FSW) relies on direct tool–workpiece contact and a fixed pin geometry, making pronounced out-of-plane deviations particularly challenging. This study investigates the compensation of severe LM by multi-pin wire-based friction stir welding (W-FSW) of 2 mm thick EN AW-6082-T6 aluminium sheets using continuously supplied EN AW-6082 filler wire. LM values of 1.0 and 1.5 mm, corresponding to 50% and 75% of the base material thickness, were introduced with the elevated sheet positioned on either the advancing or retreating side. Welding without filler material resulted in pronounced bonding defects and lack of penetration. In contrast, continuous filler-material supply enabled all investigated LM configurations to be compensated and resulted in substantially improved joint consolidation. Tensile strengths between 198.8 and 210.0 MPa were achieved, compared with 208.71 MPa for a reference W-FSW joint without LM. All tensile specimens fractured outside the joining zone within the softened heat-affected zone, showing that the compensated LM region did not control the final fracture location under the investigated tensile-test conditions. Multi-pin W-FSW therefore demonstrates a substantially increased tolerance towards severe out-of-plane joint deviations.线性错边（LM）可能由尺寸公差、定位误差、构件刚度不足以及装夹约束引起，在大型焊接结构中尤为常见。尽管填充材料辅助熔焊在适应此类几何偏差方面具有一定的灵活性，但常规搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）依赖于工具与工件的直接接触以及固定的搅拌针几何形状，因此显著的面外偏差尤其难以处理。本研究采用多搅拌针送丝式搅拌摩擦焊（W-FSW），通过连续供给EN AW-6082填充焊丝，对2 mm厚EN AW-6082-T6铝板的严重LM进行补偿研究。设置了1.0和1.5 mm的LM，分别对应母材厚度的50%和75%，并将较高侧板材分别置于前进侧或后退侧。不使用填充材料进行焊接时，出现了明显的结合缺陷和未焊透。相比之下，连续供给填充材料能够补偿所有受研究的LM配置，并显著改善接头的固结质量。所得接头的抗拉强度为198.8至210.0 MPa，而无LM的W-FSW参考接头的抗拉强度为208.71 MPa。所有拉伸试样均在连接区外的软化热影响区内断裂，表明在所研究的拉伸试验条件下，经补偿的LM区域并未控制最终断裂位置。因此，多搅拌针W-FSW表现出对接头严重面外偏差显著增强的容忍能力。
