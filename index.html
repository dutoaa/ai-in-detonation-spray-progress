Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于预测高密度聚乙烯搅拌摩擦焊焊接质量的计算建模与机器学习方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. These findings provide process-driven design guidelines for developing robust HDPE joints and demonstrate how integrating computational modeling with machine learning enables efficient optimization, offering valuable insights into the key characteristics of the FSW process in thermoplastics and supporting its broader adoption in industrial applications.搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）已被公认为一种连接热塑性材料的有效技术。本研究综合采用计算建模与机器学习方法，对高密度聚乙烯（HDPE）搅拌摩擦焊中的焊接温度、竖直轴向力、材料流动和缺陷形成等特征进行预测，并通过实验结果加以验证。研究建立了三维耦合欧拉–拉格朗日（CEL）数值模型，以模拟不同转速、焊接速度及底部预热条件下的HDPE焊接过程。通过实验数据对CEL数值模型进行了验证，结果表明，该模型在预测孔洞形成、热响应和力学响应方面与实验结果高度吻合。结果显示，底面预热可有效减小温度梯度，并抑制竖直方向力的波动。定量而言，60 °C底面预热使压入峰值力从1600 N降至接近1100 N，并使峰值温度从未预热时的70 °C升至约100 °C，从而减小厚度方向的温度梯度，并使轴向力分布趋于稳定。同时，利用实验数据训练了两个多层感知机人工神经网络（ANN），用于预测温度和轴向力随时间的响应，其R2介于0.91与0.99之间，表明模型在所测试的FSW参数空间内具有较高的准确性和较强的泛化能力。CEL计算模拟准确预测了焊接区域形貌，包括搅拌区、热机械影响区和热影响区；而结合CEL与ANN的框架能够快速生成工艺图，标示存在缺陷和无缺陷的焊接工艺窗口。在所测试条件下，最优条件为1500 rpm、30 mm/min及60 °C预热；在1000 rpm且不预热时，由于形成隧道状缺陷，焊接性较差。这些发现为制备可靠的HDPE接头提供了基于工艺的设计指导，并展示了计算建模与机器学习的集成如何实现高效优化，为理解热塑性材料FSW过程的关键特征提供了有价值的见解，同时支持该技术在工业应用中的更广泛采用。

WC-10Co-4Cr cemented carbide coating has the advantages of superior wear property and corrosion resistance. WC-10Co-4Cr coatings were prepared on 304 stainless steel substrates by different thermal spray technologies including atmospheric plasma spraying (APS), detonation spraying (DS), high velocity oxy-fuel spraying (HVOFS), and high velocity air-fuel spraying (HVAFS), respectively. The microstructure, tribological properties and corrosion resistances of WC-10Co-4Cr coatings were systematically investigated and compared. The results showed that the Vickers hardness of cemented coatings prepared by APS, DS, HVOF, and HVAF, which were all higher than those of 304 stainless steel, were 771.4 HV, 954.2 HV, 1166.2 HV, and 1162.2 HV, respectively. The volume wear rate of 304 stainless steel was about (36.82±0.80)×10-5 mm3/N·m. The volume wear rates of APS, DS, HVOF, and HVAF coatings, which were all lower than that of 304 stainless steel, were about (7.93±0.30)×10-5, (1.51±0.20)×10-5, (1.17±0.10)×10-5, and (1.10±0.01)×10-5 mm3/N·m, respectively. Spalling, microcracks perpendicular to the friction direction, and grooves parallel to the friction direction can be found in the wear marks of WC-10Co-4Cr coatings. Abrasive wear and fatigue wear are the main wear mechanisms of the cemented coatings. During the wear test, the carbide particles in DS, HVOF and HVAF coatings gradually became the main body bearing the friction load. Then the wear resistance of the latter three coatings was all superior to that of APS. Moreover, HVOF and HVAF coatings have better wear performances. For corrosion resistance from high to low were HVAF coating, DS one, HVOF one and APS one, respectively. The HVAF coating demonstrated the highest corrosion potential (-0.37±0.01 VSCE), smallest corrosion current density (1.75±0.02 μA/cm²), and largest corrosion resistance (20438.5 Ω) here. Molten pool dynamic behavior and arc characteristics in laser-TIG hybrid welding of Invar steelInvar钢激光-TIG复合焊接中的熔池动态行为与电弧特性

Sen WU, Fei ZHAO, Shuili GONG, Lifeng MA, Haiyan ZHAO, Wei ZHANG

Invar steel has an extremely low coefficient of thermal expansion, making it widely used in the manufacturing of molds for aerospace composite materials. Most existing studies focus on process aspects, lacking in-depth analysis of the fluid dynamics behavior of the molten pool. Therefore, numerical simulation and high-speed photography were employed to analyze the effects of different process parameters on the molten pool morphology evolution and arc characteristics during laser-TIG hybrid welding of Invar steel, which provides crucial theoretical support for the optimization of the Invar steel welding process. The results indicate that within the laser power range of 5 ~ 6 kW, the increased power leads to greater recoil pressure, which promotes a larger penetration depth and keyhole dimension, intensifies the overall molten pool flow, and makes the arc prone to being attracted by the laser and deflected at a high power. Within the welding speed range of 0.5 ~ 0.8 m/min, a higher speed reduces the heat input, leading to a decreased penetration depth and weld width, and an increased flow velocity and cooling rate of the molten pool; additionally, the arc is prone to deformation at a high speed. Within the welding current range of 150 ~ 170 A, the increased current enhances the arc force, promoting the expansion of weld width and intensifying the molten pool convection; furthermore, the arc is more stable at a high current.Invar钢具有极低的热膨胀系数，因此广泛用于航空航天复合材料模具的制造。现有研究大多侧重于工艺方面，缺乏对熔池流体动力学行为的深入分析。因此，采用数值模拟和高速摄影，分析了不同工艺参数对Invar钢激光-TIG复合焊接过程中熔池形貌演化和电弧特性的影响，为Invar钢焊接工艺优化提供了关键的理论支持。结果表明，在5 ~ 6 kW的激光功率范围内，功率增大使反冲压力增大，从而增加熔深和小孔尺寸，增强熔池整体流动，且在高功率下，电弧易受激光吸引而发生偏转。在0.5 ~ 0.8 m/min的焊接速度范围内，速度提高会降低热输入，导致熔深和焊缝宽度减小，熔池流速和冷却速率增大；此外，高速下电弧易发生变形。在150 ~ 170 A的焊接电流范围内，电流增大使电弧力增强，促进焊缝宽度增大并强化熔池对流；此外，高电流下电弧更加稳定。

Interpretable ensemble learning for tensile strength prediction and welding process window optimization of friction stir-welded 7N01 aluminum alloy用于搅拌摩擦焊7N01铝合金抗拉强度预测与焊接工艺窗口优化的可解释集成学习

Xiaocui Wu, Yingjie Zhao, Huijie Zhang, Qi Cheng, Limao Wei, Yuwang Liu

This study aims to achieve high-accuracy prediction of the ultimate tensile strength (UTS) of friction stir-welded A7N01P-T4 aluminum alloy joints, elucidate the effects of welding parameters on UTS, and optimize the welding process window. Rotational speed, welding speed, and shoulder plunge depth were used as input variables, with UTS as the output. Machine learning models were developed using 27 experimental samples and evaluated by leave-one-out cross-validation. Comparison of decision tree, K-nearest neighbors, and Gaussian process regression identifies decision tree as the best baseline model, with an R 2 of 0.905. An ensemble learning strategy was subsequently introduced to construct a gradient boosting decision tree, increasing R 2 to 0.966, and grid search optimization of its key hyperparameters further improves R 2 to 0.978. SHapley Additive exPlanations analysis shows that the importance of the welding parameters follows the order of welding speed, rotational speed, and shoulder plunge depth, and the identified parameter effects agree well with the experimental results and observed microstructural evolution. The optimized model predicts fully feasible ranges of all the welding parameters for a joint efficiency of at least 90%, and the shoulder plunge depth acts as a key parameter for further refining the high-strength welding process window. These results demonstrate that the proposed approach not only improves the accuracy of UTS prediction but also reveals welding parameter effects and enables optimization of the welding process window, providing a quantitative basis for selecting FSW parameters.本研究旨在高精度预测搅拌摩擦焊A7N01P-T4铝合金接头的极限抗拉强度（UTS），阐明焊接参数对UTS的影响，并优化焊接工艺窗口。以旋转速度、焊接速度和轴肩下压量为输入变量，以UTS为输出变量。利用27个实验样本建立机器学习模型，并通过留一交叉验证进行评估。通过比较决策树、K近邻和高斯过程回归，确定决策树为最佳基准模型，其R²为0.905。随后引入集成学习策略，构建梯度提升决策树，将R²提高至0.966，并通过网格搜索优化其关键超参数，将R²进一步提高至0.978。SHapley Additive exPlanations（SHAP）分析表明，焊接参数的重要性由高到低依次为焊接速度、旋转速度和轴肩下压量，且所揭示的参数影响与实验结果及观察到的微观组织演变高度一致。优化后的模型预测了接头效率至少达到90%时所有焊接参数的完整可行范围，其中轴肩下压量是进一步细化高强度焊接工艺窗口的关键参数。这些结果表明，所提出的方法不仅提高了UTS预测精度，还揭示了焊接参数的影响并实现了焊接工艺窗口优化，为选择搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）参数提供了定量依据。

Long-term corrosion behaviors and wear behavior of Fe-based amorphous coatings in different humidity environments铁基非晶涂层在不同湿度环境下的长期腐蚀行为与磨损行为

Shuai Cui, Haimin Zhai, Zihan Feng, Xuqiang Li, Wensheng Li, Xiangjuan Fan

Fe-based amorphous coatings (Fe-based AMCs) were successfully prepared on Q235 carbon steel by detonation spray under different spraying parameters. The corrosion and tribology behaviors of coatings were systematically tested. The droplets show disk-like morphology with the highest circularity and expansion factor, which is crucial to reduce the defects of coatings and to form fine-lamellar-structures coatings with dense microstructure. A higher incidence of porosity defects in the coating is associated with reduced long-term corrosion resistance. Moreover, the tribo-chemical reactions between Si3N4 balls and water molecules play a significant role in wear behaviors for coating in different humidity conditions. The friction coefficient and wear rate of coatings decrease as the humidity conditions increase.采用不同喷涂参数，通过爆炸喷涂在Q235碳钢上成功制备了铁基非晶涂层（Fe-based AMCs）。系统测试了涂层的腐蚀与摩擦学行为。液滴呈圆盘状形貌，具有最高的圆度和铺展因子，这对于减少涂层缺陷以及形成具有致密微观组织的细片层结构涂层至关重要。涂层中孔隙缺陷越多，其长期耐腐蚀性越低。此外，Si3N4球与水分子之间的摩擦化学反应对涂层在不同湿度条件下的磨损行为具有重要影响。涂层的摩擦系数和磨损率随湿度升高而降低。

Surface Characteristics of Titanium Based Coatings Obtained by Detonation Spraying Under Various Process Conditions不同工艺条件下爆炸喷涂制备的钛基涂层的表面特性

Vyacheslav Sirota, Sergey Savotchenko, Valeriya Strokova, Daniil Podgornyi, Marina Kovaleva

The surface properties of metal-ceramic coatings based on titanium dioxide are described in dependence on the detonation spraying conditions. It is found that such properties as surface roughness, surface thickness and its hydrophobicity can be controlled in the production process by selecting certain values of the technological parameters of the spraying process. The optimal values of the technological parameters of detonation spraying, ensuring maximum hydrophobicity of the produced coatings are determined. The roughness of the coating surface and the coating thickness depend on the speed of the nozzle passage in accordance with the inverse power law. The roughness and the contact angle depend on spray distance in accordance with a parabolic law. New equations are obtained that can be useful for predicting the characteristics of the coating surface, as well as for determining the optimal mode of spraying the coating, ensuring its best hydrophobicity.本文描述了二氧化钛基金属陶瓷涂层的表面性能与爆炸喷涂条件的关系。研究发现，通过选择喷涂工艺参数的特定值，可以在制备过程中控制表面粗糙度、表面层厚度及其疏水性等性能。确定了使所制备涂层具有最大疏水性的爆炸喷涂工艺参数最优值。涂层表面粗糙度和涂层厚度与喷嘴移动速度之间符合反幂律关系。粗糙度和接触角与喷涂距离之间符合抛物线关系。获得了新的方程，可用于预测涂层表面特性，以及确定使涂层具有最佳疏水性的最优喷涂工况。
