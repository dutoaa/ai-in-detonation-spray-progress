Computational and machine learning modelling approaches for weld quality predictions in friction stir welding of high-density polyethylene用于高密度聚乙烯搅拌摩擦焊焊接质量预测的计算与机器学习建模方法

A. Alhourani, I. Barsoum, J. Sheikh-Ahmad, S. Deveci

Friction stir welding (FSW) has gained recognition as an effective technique for joining thermoplastic materials. This study presents a comprehensive investigation to predict FSW characteristics such as weld temperatures, vertical axial forces, material flow and defect formation in FSW of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) using both computational and machine learning approaches, validated by experimental results. A three-dimensional Coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian (CEL) numerical model is developed to simulate the HDPE welding process under varying process parameters of rotational and welding speeds, and bottom preheating conditions. The CEL numerical model is validated against experimental data, showing markedly good agreement in predicting void formation, thermal and mechanical responses. Results indicate that preheating the bottom surface proved effective in reducing thermal gradients and stabilizing vertical force fluctuations. Quantitatively, bottom-surface preheating at 60 °C reduced peak plunge force from 1600 N close to 1100 N and raised peak temperatures from 70 °C (without preheat) to about 100 °C, hence smoothing through-thickness gradients and stabilizing the axial force distribution. In parallel, two multilayer perceptron artificial neural networks (ANNs) are trained on experimental data to predict temporal temperature and axial force responses with R2 between 0.91 and 0.99, demonstrating high accuracy and strong generalization across the tested FSW parameter space. The computational CEL simulations accurately predicted weld zone morphology, including stir zone, thermo-mechanically affected zone and heat-affected zone, however the combined CEL–ANN framework enables rapid generation of process maps that highlights defective and defect-free welding windows, with optimal conditions obtained at 1500 rpm and 30 mm/min with 60 °C preheating, and poor weldability observed at 1000 rpm without preheating due to wormhole formation. These findings provide process-driven design guidelines for developing robust HDPE joints and demonstrate how integrating computational modeling with machine learning enables efficient optimization, offering valuable insights into the key characteristics of the FSW process in thermoplastics and supporting its broader adoption in industrial applications.搅拌摩擦焊（FSW）已被公认为连接热塑性材料的一种有效技术。本研究综合采用计算与机器学习方法，对高密度聚乙烯（HDPE）搅拌摩擦焊中的焊接温度、垂直轴向力、材料流动和缺陷形成等特征进行预测，并通过实验结果加以验证。研究建立了三维耦合欧拉–拉格朗日（CEL）数值模型，以模拟不同转速、焊接速度和底部预热条件下的HDPE焊接过程。通过实验数据验证了CEL数值模型，其在孔洞形成以及热响应和力学响应的预测方面均与实验结果表现出良好的一致性。结果表明，底面预热能够有效减小温度梯度，并抑制垂直力波动。定量而言，60 °C底面预热使峰值压入力从1600 N降至接近1100 N，并使峰值温度从未预热时的70 °C升至约100 °C，从而减缓厚度方向的温度梯度，并使轴向力分布更加稳定。同时，利用实验数据训练了两个多层感知机人工神经网络（ANN），用于预测温度和轴向力随时间的响应，R2介于0.91和0.99之间，表明其在所测试的FSW参数空间内具有较高的准确性和较强的泛化能力。CEL计算模拟准确预测了焊接区域形貌，包括搅拌区、热机械影响区和热影响区；而CEL–ANN组合框架能够快速生成工艺图，标示有缺陷和无缺陷的焊接工艺窗口。在所测试条件中，1500 rpm、30 mm/min和60 °C预热条件下获得了最佳结果，而在1000 rpm且不预热时，由于形成虫孔缺陷，焊接性较差。这些发现为制备可靠的HDPE接头提供了工艺导向的设计指南，并展示了计算建模与机器学习相结合如何实现高效优化，为认识热塑性材料FSW过程的关键特征提供了有价值的见解，也为其在工业应用中的更广泛采用提供了支持。

From Splat Spreading to Coating Densification: Substrate Thermal Effects in Plasma-Sprayed NiCrAl-TiB2 Coatings从扁平粒子铺展到涂层致密化：等离子喷涂NiCrAl-TiB2涂层中的基体热效应

Yuxiang Wang, Ze-Xin Wang, Ekaterina A. Gridasova, Sheng Lu, Liang‐Yu Chen

Controlling molten droplet deposition and coating densification is crucial for improving the surface properties of lightweight aluminum alloys; however, the relationship between substrate temperature, splat spreading–solidification behavior, and the resulting microstructure of atmospheric plasma-sprayed (APS) composite coatings remains insufficiently understood. The key novelty of this work is to establish a multiscale relationship between the temperature-dependent behavior of individual splats and the subsequent densification and mechanical properties of the bulk coating. Establishing how individual deposition units evolve into a dense coating structure is essential for the rational control of coating microstructural and mechanical integrity. This study investigated the droplet deposition behavior, microstructural evolution, and hardness of NiCrAl-10 wt.% TiB2 coatings fabricated on 6061-T6 aluminum alloy substrates at substrate temperatures of 230, 330, and 430 °C. Increasing substrate temperature promoted splat spreading and morphological continuity by delaying solidification and extending the liquid-state lifetime. On polished substrates, the average splat diameter increased from 47.82 ± 5.4 to 57.54 ± 3.1 μm, while coating porosity decreased from 1.48 ± 0.42% to 0.55 ± 0.26%. Meanwhile, microhardness increased from 317.7 ± 3.9 to 336.3 ± 3.2 HV0.1. The dominant γ-Ni(Cr,Al) phase remained unchanged. These results establish a direct mechanistic link between substrate temperature, individual splat evolution, and bulk coating properties, providing a mechanistic basis for optimizing APS fabricated NiCrAl-10 wt.% TiB2 coatings.控制熔融液滴沉积和涂层致密化对于改善轻质铝合金的表面性能至关重要；然而，基体温度、扁平粒子的铺展–凝固行为与大气等离子喷涂（APS）复合涂层最终微观组织之间的关系仍缺乏充分认识。本研究的主要创新在于，建立了单个扁平粒子的温度依赖行为与后续整体涂层的致密化及力学性能之间的多尺度关系。明确单个沉积单元如何演变为致密涂层结构，对于合理调控涂层的微观组织完整性和力学完整性至关重要。本研究考察了在230、330和430 °C基体温度下，于6061-T6铝合金基体上制备的NiCrAl-10 wt.% TiB2涂层的液滴沉积行为、微观组织演变及硬度。提高基体温度通过延缓凝固和延长液态持续时间，促进了扁平粒子的铺展并改善了形貌连续性。在抛光基体上，扁平粒子的平均直径由47.82 ± 5.4 μm增至57.54 ± 3.1 μm，而涂层孔隙率由1.48 ± 0.42%降至0.55 ± 0.26%。同时，显微硬度由317.7 ± 3.9 HV0.1增至336.3 ± 3.2 HV0.1。主导相γ-Ni(Cr,Al)保持不变。这些结果确立了基体温度、单个扁平粒子演变与整体涂层性能之间的直接机制联系，为优化APS制备的NiCrAl-10 wt.% TiB2涂层提供了机理依据。

WC-10Co-4Cr coatings were prepared on 304 stainless steel substrates by different thermal spray technologies including atmospheric plasma spraying (APS), detonation spraying (DS), high velocity oxy-fuel spraying (HVOFS), and high velocity air-fuel spraying (HVAFS), respectively. The microstructure, tribological properties and corrosion resistances of WC-10Co-4Cr coatings were systematically investigated and compared. The results showed that the Vickers hardness of cemented coatings prepared by APS, DS, HVOF, and HVAF, which were all higher than those of 304 stainless steel, were 771.4 HV, 954.2 HV, 1166.2 HV, and 1162.2 HV, respectively. The volume wear rate of 304 stainless steel was about (36.82±0.80)×10-5 mm3/N·m. The volume wear rates of APS, DS, HVOF, and HVAF coatings, which were all lower than that of 304 stainless steel, were about (7.93±0.30)×10-5, (1.51±0.20)×10-5, (1.17±0.10)×10-5, and (1.10±0.01)×10-5 mm3/N·m, respectively. Abrasive wear and fatigue wear are the main wear mechanisms of the cemented coatings. During the wear test, the carbide particles in DS, HVOF and HVAF coatings gradually became the main body bearing the friction load. Then the wear resistance of the latter three coatings was all superior to that of APS. Moreover, HVOF and HVAF coatings have better wear performances. This study investigates the microstructural and high-temperature oxidation behaviour of SA516 boiler steel coated with Ni-20Cr, Ni-20Cr+40TiC, and Ni-20Cr+40TiN using High Velocity Oxygen Fuel (HVOF) and Plasma Spray (PS) techniques. Molten pool dynamic behavior and arc characteristics in laser-TIG hybrid welding of Invar steelInvar钢激光-TIG复合焊接中的熔池动态行为与电弧特性

Sen WU, Fei ZHAO, Shuili GONG, Lifeng MA, Haiyan ZHAO, Wei ZHANG

Invar steel has an extremely low coefficient of thermal expansion, making it widely used in the manufacturing of molds for aerospace composite materials. Most existing studies focus on process aspects, lacking in-depth analysis of the fluid dynamics behavior of the molten pool. Therefore, numerical simulation and high-speed photography were employed to analyze the effects of different process parameters on the molten pool morphology evolution and arc characteristics during laser-TIG hybrid welding of Invar steel, which provides crucial theoretical support for the optimization of the Invar steel welding process. The results indicate that within the laser power range of 5 ~ 6 kW, the increased power leads to greater recoil pressure, which promotes a larger penetration depth and keyhole dimension, intensifies the overall molten pool flow, and makes the arc prone to being attracted by the laser and deflected at a high power. Within the welding speed range of 0.5 ~ 0.8 m/min, a higher speed reduces the heat input, leading to a decreased penetration depth and weld width, and an increased flow velocity and cooling rate of the molten pool; additionally, the arc is prone to deformation at a high speed.
