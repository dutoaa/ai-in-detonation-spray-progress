## Outlook

The next step for AI in detonation spray and detonation spray coating is likely not a fully autonomous system, but a hybrid workflow that combines physics-based spray modeling, microstructure-aware feature extraction, and optimization over a small number of high-value experimental runs. The July 2026 literature suggests that the enabling pieces are already emerging in adjacent thermal spray and welding domains, especially in monitoring, parameter optimization, and process-structure-property mapping. For detonation spray specifically, progress will depend on building shared datasets, defining coating-quality targets more precisely, and validating AI recommendations against long-term service performance such as oxidation, wear, adhesion, and thermal shock resistance.
